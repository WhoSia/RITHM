# Source grouping and leakage audit
Original authenticated ZIP contains data_task_all_switch_230705.csv; after author-defined exclusions (trt <=3, session not 2 or 101, section<=4), join existing previously held-out individual one-step choice forecasts using (trt,session,section,Group,ID,Period) with one_to_one cardinality. Each (trt,session,section,Group,Period) contains exactly six choices. This yields 5,760 group-period rows from 144 group-blocks and 24 whole sessions. No group-period with fewer/more than six was accepted. Counts and cost are from actual simultaneous choices; individual forecasts conditioned only on **past** realized participant history. Joining at a current period for scoring does not mean current other-players' actions are visible at prediction time.
