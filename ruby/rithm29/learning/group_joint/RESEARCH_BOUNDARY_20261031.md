# Interpretation and next tests
Model losses: EWA predicts too many switchers on average but gives a lower seven-class Brier than the empirical count benchmarks. A single scalar mean-squared error, categorical Brier and logloss answer different questions. The weak EWA−baseline logloss contrast has a session bootstrap interval crossing zero; avoid model rank triumphalism.
A promising 2.10 research object is a **joint law of gross turnover S and net flow ΔN**, not simply marginal distribution of N_A. The exact cancellation theorem provides an observable statistic with no latent assumptions. Future tests: condition pair-cancellation rate on prior congestion N_{t−1}, type of information and time; compare independent switch forecasts against observed direction-specific U,D joint distribution; separate marginal switch probability calibration from conditional dependence using training-only calibrated models. No human recruitment. Additional datasets not yet required.
