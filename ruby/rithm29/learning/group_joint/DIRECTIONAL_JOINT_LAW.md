# Joint directional-switch distribution
For each group-period, previous A membership partitions the six actors into n_prev incumbent A and 6-n_prev incumbent B. If person i has one-step EWA forecast p_i=P(next A | source-observable past), then an A incumbent switches downward with probability 1-p_i and B incumbent switches upward with probability p_i. Convolve each direction's Bernoulli polynomial to get P(D=d) and P(U=u); the **independence assumption** yields P(U=u,D=d)=P(U=u)P(D=d). This automatically respects feasible supports 0<=U<=6-n_prev and 0<=D<=n_prev. Do not pool switches before distinguishing direction.
