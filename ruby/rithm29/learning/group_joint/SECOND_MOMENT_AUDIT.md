# Welfare requires distributional calibration
The independent-convolution forecast average expected group costs were base=4.6696, Markov=4.6429, EWA=4.6763, SSM-person-q=4.8573, versus actual 4.50243056. This discrepancy reflects both predicted count mean bias and variance; source formula E[C]=4+(Var(N)+(E N-4)^2)/2 and its implementation independently checked for every group-period. These naive independent aggregation models systematically overpredict pooled cost. This is not proof of dependence, because individual probability bias and other miscalibration may also explain it. Need calibration decomposition and conditional synchrony tests rather than assuming correlation sign.
