# Numeric roundoff boundary audit
The first test after FFT-based mass convolution found outputs as large as 1+1e-15 in deterministic extreme-history cases. The theoretical probability cannot exceed 1. The code clips negative FFT density to zero, renormalizes densities, and clips P(A) to [0,1]. Independently computed q=1 values agree within 6.7e-16, and exhaustive 2-action, 4-draw combinatorial enumeration agrees to 1e-10. The difference between 'mathematically exact finite distribution' and floating point numerical realization is recorded here to prevent overstated machine-exactness.
