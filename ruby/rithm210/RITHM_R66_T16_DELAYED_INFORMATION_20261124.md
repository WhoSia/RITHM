## 2. Exact model and boundary
K=0..5 other actors on A. Myopic gain A->B =3K-11, B->A =11-3K. Predictive transition T(k,j)=rho*1[k=j]+(1-rho)*b[j], b=Binomial(5,2/3). This is an exogenous subjective transition, NOT the true endogenous six-agent transition. With starting prior b, risk neutrality, threshold zero, E[g_A(K_next)|K=k]=-1+rho(3k-10); B is its negative. For 0<=rho<=1/5, expected gains have a constant weak sign across k and perfect disclosure about K has zero decision value. For rho>1/5 both signs occur with strictly positive b mass; perfect disclosure over blind has positive value. Payoff is a garbling of past K; exact continuous piecewise integration decomposes total information value into payoff increment and incremental complete past-state disclosure. At rho=.5 A increments .039506/.158025, B .079012/.118519. At rho=1 A .158025/.697942, B .316049/.539918. The additional disclosure increment is verified positive just above the boundary in this model; zero total value at/below it includes the tie. No human persistence parameter is estimated.

## 3. Chronological Bayesian filter rival
Condition on known own route as a controlled observation, update by payoff likelihood, optionally incorporate genuinely disclosed past K, and propagate through T for the next decision. At rho=4/5, A-route payoff histories 14,9 versus 0,9 give next expected gain -311/55 versus 11/5, with opposite strict optimal actions. At rho=0 the same last observation gives the same posterior and prediction regardless of prior history. Thus history dependence can emerge without habit, reference preference or fixed type; it is not proof that subjects use Bayesian filtering. The current model does not close the feedback loop with other actors' decisions; no 40-period welfare prediction is claimed. Future T17-A must implement six actors' own information histories with a separate true transition and subjective predictor, compare against an exact small-horizon oracle, and report paired simulated welfare across matched draws.
