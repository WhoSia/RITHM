## 3. Exactly sharp classical dependence bounds

Write \`X_i=1\` when person \`i\` changes route, with marginal \`p_i=P(X_i=1)\`. The event that all six stay equals no person changing. Elementary union-probability bounds yield, for *every* six-variable joint law with these exact marginals,

\[
\max(0,1-\sum_{i=1}^{6}p_i)\ \leq\ P(X_1=\cdots=X_6=0)\
\leq\ 1-\max_{1\leq i\leq6}p_i.
\]

The bounds are SHARP: upper endpoint is attained by nested switching events on a common uniform random number; lower endpoint by successive nonoverlapping intervals on a unit circle, wrapping when cumulative marginal mass exceeds 1. The public Python exact \`Fraction\` verifier constructs both six-person coupling distributions, partitions at rational endpoints into <=64 possible indicator masks, checks normalisation and ALL six marginal equalities, and verifies equality at the corresponding bound. This is an exact finite probability construction, **not a novel theorem**.

Averaged over the 530 observed old-A4 Partial transitions, the **legal bridge's FORECAST marginal probabilities** imply:

| Bound averaged over forecast individual switching marginals | Probability |
|---|---:|
| Mean pointwise Fréchet lower bound | **0.074166** |
| Mean pointwise Fréchet upper bound | **0.562960** |
| Empirical all-stay rate | **0.300000** |

The interval is broad enough to contain actual .30 and the independent model's .23349. Crucial qualification: **these are bounds conditional on taking the fitted \`p_i\` as exact**. The R63B and R63C empirical marginal calibration errors show this premise is not itself proven; therefore the reported interval is a hypothetical feasible-coupling band, not a certified confidence interval or an identified set for a causal group mechanism.

## 5. Research direction — R63C-P1 before more latent variables

R63C-P0 confirms that a substantial part of apparent group all-stay discrepancy could be a miscalibrated legally allowed personal switch law, but the analyst-only conditioning means this is **diagnostic, not a deployable model**. It also demonstrates the large mathematical slack in possible group dependence even with fixed person marginals.

Next compare:
1. Legally admissible personal feedback (own previous payoff, previous route and tenure) with finer but explicitly support-bounded training strata, frozen before heldout evaluation.
2. Strongly controlled group common shocks or latent pauses *only after* lawful single-person alternatives are calibrated; require improvements in joint (U,D), A4 all-stay and autonomous 40-period cost simultaneously, not cherry-picked objectives.
3. Partial mixed-info subgroup margins and treatment transfer, held-out whole original sessions; freeze the original 17-session exploratory evidence and secure a truly new independent cohort or a preregistered data collection before confirmatory statements.

**HOLD**: personal habit causality, strategy/coordination identification, causal treatment-induced welfare, actual switching resource price \`κ\`, general novel theorem, reliable external replication and any information policy recommendation.
