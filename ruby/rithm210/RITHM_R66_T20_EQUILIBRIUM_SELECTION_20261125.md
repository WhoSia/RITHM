## T20-C exact joint stability/escape obstruction on k in [0,2]

For n=5, p_A=[1,1,1,(2-k)/6,(2-k)/6], p_B=[(2+3k)/6] similarly permits leaving n=5 throughout that interval. For M>=2 the S and all probabilities are affine in k; enumerate each integer count pattern and intersect all exact linear inequalities over [0,2]. Maximum upper endpoint of any genuine-mixing feasible pattern is 4/3 for both n=4 and n=5. M=1 can occur only at k=1 or 2 in this interval (integer pure-A count = theta); at k=2 endpoints both stay in original occupancy. Pure profiles at k>1 cannot leave n=4 or n=5: any downward movement forces at least one old A to choose B with others' K<=4, gain<=1-k<0; any upward movement forces an old B to choose A with others' K>=4, gain<=-1-k<0. Thus for 4/3<k<=2 all independent equilibria preserve either initial occupancy. On [0,2] there is no common k that ensures n=4 stable under ALL independent stage equilibria while allowing n=5 to escape under ANY. This does not rule out coordination, correlated strategies, individualized friction or dynamic commitment; no novelty claim.
