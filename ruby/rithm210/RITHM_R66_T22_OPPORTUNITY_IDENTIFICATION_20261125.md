## A. Exact opportunity identification
Conditional on current K=k and common opportunity E, E=0 implies K_next=k. With q=P(E=1|K=k)>0, P=(1-q)I+q A at row level. For observed row P, q in [1-Pkk,1] intersect positive q. A_j=(P_j-(1-q)1[j=k])/q. Every feasible q gives a valid row and same observations. Active expected gain g_A=g(k)+(g_P-g(k))/q, monotone in q. Hence sharp endpoints. If Pkk=1 and q>0, A=delta_k. If q=0, active row not identified. Example K=5, P=(delta_5+delta_3)/2, route B: q=1/2 implies active gain +2; q=1 implies -1. Same observed row, opposite active decision. This is row-local, not claim of simultaneous rational-equilibrium compatibility across all actors and rows.

## C. Imperfect proxy identification
Observe joint rows J_z(j), Z binary opportunity proxy. E=0 implies identity. If r=P(K_next!=k)>0 and Z independent of next state given E,current k, alpha=P(Z=1|change,k) identified, check J1(j)=alpha*P(j) for every off-diagonal j. m=P(Z=1|k). Unknown false-positive rate beta in [0,1] implies r<=q<=min(1,m/alpha,(1-m)/(1-alpha)), omit zero denominators. If beta known and alpha!=beta, q=(m-beta)/(alpha-beta) subject to feasibility. Example k=5, P3=P5=.5, alpha=.9,m=.5, q in [.5,5/9], B gain in [7/5,2], resolves positive sign vs [-1,2] absent proxy. No-change case excluded from proxy estimator.

## D. Differential-error sensitivity
Relax independence: average sensitivity alpha0 among active nonmovers within delta of alpha among movers; arbitrary tags across changed states and arbitrary inactive beta. Let s=J1(k), L=max(0,alpha-delta), U=min(1,alpha+delta). Feasibility iff (q-r)L<=s<=(q-r)U+1-q. Thus q upper min(1,r+s/L,r+(1-r-s)/(1-U)) with zero denominators omitted. Construct tagged active-stay mass max(L*(q-r),s-(1-q)), then residual inactive masses; every endpoint attained. Example above sign strictly positive iff delta<.7, weak at .7, ambiguous beyond; delta=1 restores no-proxy region. Exact probability-level sensitivity result, not finite-sample confidence or calibrated empirical error. Inactive identity is still a strong assumption; unknown background drift may invalidate this result. q is state-local; common q across rows could tighten bounds.
