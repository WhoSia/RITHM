## B. Conditional ordering — strengthened rival reverses interpretation
For N binary switches, condition on total M under exchangeability: expected adjacent both-switch count M(M-1)/N. This is valid for constant individual iid switching rate mixtures but not arbitrary stationary strategies. Stronger null: each actor has homogeneous two-state Markov transitions with arbitrary route-specific rates. Condition on first route and four directed transition counts; all feasible paths have equal likelihood (when conditioning event has positive probability). This fixes route visit totals T_a and run counts R_a, alternating run labels and terminal route; lengths per route are uniformly distributed over positive compositions of T_a into R_a parts. An adjacent pair of switches corresponds to an internal run of length one. For internal runs of route a, probability length one is (R_a-1)/(T_a-1) if T_a>1; equals one for T_a=1. Internal count excludes endpoint runs; zero internal count contributes zero. Sum across routes. Exact expectation verified by enumerating every binary path of lengths 3..9, grouping by starting route and transition counts (1016 assertions); simpler exchangeability expectation checked by exhaustive combinations lengths 2..9.
Whole sample 864 trajectories x38 adjacent pairs: observed 3569; simple expected 3223.6923076923076; Markov conditional expected 3754.1165212010974. Session-mean observed-minus-expected per pair .011110954915779478 under simple null, -.005270288894009567 under Markov, positive 22 vs 7 of 24 sessions.
