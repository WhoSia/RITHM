## Exact six-agent Markov negative control

Let N[t] be the 0..6 occupancy on route A, with arm-specific independent action flips at n: A->B probability a(n) and B->A probability b(n). The exact 7x7 transition P[n,m] is a binomial convolution. For Delta[t]=N[t+1]-N[t] and f(n)=E[Delta[t]|N[t]=n], write

Cov(Delta[t],Delta[t+1]) = Cov(Delta[t],f(N[t+1])) + Cov(Delta[t],Delta[t+1]-f(N[t+1])).

Under correctly specified Markov dynamics the second covariance is zero. Even if occupancies were i.i.d. Binomial(6,2/3), the *raw* covariance would equal -Var(N)=-4/3. This is purely a differencing artifact and not anti-coordination. Exact rational implementation `ruby/rithm210/p3/r66_t10_state_null.py` and ten tests locally passed.
