# R39 design — How learning rules amplify or dampen congestion

Frozen before the first simulation. Use the SHA-verified 24 main Partial sessions, 48 groups, six actors and 40 rounds. Use the six R38 fold coefficients as published, without fitting to trajectory targets. The primary comparison conditions on the same observed round-one actions and payoffs and then recursively simulates rounds 2–40. Generate common independent travel values on the uniform 6.0–16.0 tenth-point grid after choice; each model experiences its own congestion and payoffs. Sensitivity uses independently initialized round-one choices at 0.5 with the same draws across rules. No held-out future choices, payoffs, values, or counts enter the simulation.

Models: fitted EWA-lite; refitted R38 compact EWA+choice, EWA+payoff, and full; SSM5 and SSM1–9 using the R37 feedback rules and one fixed draw of q per actor in SSM1–9; plus four frozen versions of the full model with the choice/payoff coefficients independently kept or set to zero. The latter factorial isolates in-model coefficient changes and permits an interaction contrast; fitted-family contrasts also incorporate recalibration of EWA. Replay check against R37 attractions and R33 features, and SSM sample checks against exact R37 one-step probabilities.

Primary endpoint: excess group congestion cost per round in periods 2–40, against the feasible four-on-A assignment. Also report cumulative cost, 2–10 and 31–40 excess cost, switch rate, severe mismatch P(|n−4|≥2), P(n=4), and split the excess cost into squared mean occupancy bias and dispersion. For each round, separate group-to-group dispersion in expected occupancy given histories from the independent Bernoulli variance within histories. All summaries are descriptive on this reused cohort. Paired Monte Carlo error reports noise from simulation draws only. Report session-level directional counts; do not label them population tests.

Stress test: warm-start replay with zero or six informed actors while holding the realized initial objective outcomes, initial choices and the common random draws fixed. This changes the simulated disclosure of period-one and subsequent outcomes. Apply frozen EWA-based policies trained on Partial; this is model transport sensitivity, not a treatment-effect estimate. Also compare compact factorial under reduced Value spread (uniform 9–13) after the same first round, keeping the EWA belief support at [6,16]; the perturbation changes the noise process without changing agents' assumed support. Keep the primary runs even if model rank changes. Do not infer psychological welfare directly from an estimated preference term.
