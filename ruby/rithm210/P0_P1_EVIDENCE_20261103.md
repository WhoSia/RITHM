## P0 theorem (exact, constructed worlds)
- [Rational Ruby generator](p0/joint_observational_equivalence.rb), [Minitest](p0/test_joint_observational_equivalence.rb).
- Let a previous route A users and b=6-a previous route B users, common fair hidden Z∈{−1,+1}, and all individual switches independent CONDITIONAL ON Z with probability p+δZ.
- Latent joint distribution P_L(U=u,D=d) = 0.5 Σ_z Bin(b,u,p+δz) Bin(a,d,p+δz).
- Direct directed SCM instead samples U~P_L(U) then D~P_L(D|U). Entire observational P(U,D) is IDENTICAL; structural do(U=u) differs because the directed SCM assigns causal influence of U on D.
- For a=3,p=1/2,δ=3/40: latent do(U=0 or 3) gives E[D]=1.5; directed gives respectively 479757/341600 (~1.4044408665) and 545043/341600 (~1.5955591335).
- Exact arithmetic Minitest local Ruby 3.3.8: **3 runs, 20 assertions, zero failures**. It is a mathematical observational-equivalence construction, not a claim of actual chronological direct effects or new theorem novelty.
