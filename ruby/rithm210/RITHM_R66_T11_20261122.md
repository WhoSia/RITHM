## Fundamental mathematical distinction

For previous aggregate change D_t, next change Y=D_(t+1), and model forecast f,

`Cov(D_t,Y)=Cov(D_t,f)+Cov(D_t,Y-f)`.

This holds for *every f* and is **not a model-validation theorem**. A smaller residual covariance can coexist with worse next-step squared error. Therefore report `MSE=E[(Y-f)^2]` and paired session uncertainty on identical evaluation events as the decisive predictive gate.
