## 1. Source-size confound in R63B

Original author-approved ZIP SHA256: `e6adce2da4d1c01455e912aa15144f192108394cbec0bd7f767e2732dcec9953`. Original source consists of 24 sessions, three treatments, 32 group-sections each, with 39 scored six-person transitions per group-section. The homogeneous No and Full treatments each contain 7,488 actor decisions in their corresponding info mask. Partial contains 3,744 decisions for uninformed and 3,744 for informed actors. Thus the R63B No+Full bridge had approximately TWICE the source decisions per actor information mask compared with the Partial-only fitted comparator. That advantage must not be attributed to transport invariance without matching source sample sizes.

## 2. R64A source-panel-matched retrospective diagnostic

For each held-out original Partial-bearing session, sample No(mask0) and Full(mask1) source actor-PANELS using design identifiers only, not observed switches. Preserve all 39 scored decisions for each selected actor; exactly match the number of other-session Partial actor-panels per mask. Fit both models without ANY participant from the held-out original session. Target = 17 original Partial-bearing sessions, 32 group-sections, 1,248 group transitions, 7,488 actor decisions. Source-person panel selection has eight fixed random seeds; first three matched subsets receive 64-rep 40-period autonomous rollouts with coupled random uniforms. No actor future outcome or Value(t) is used as a current prediction input.

## 4. Adversarial evaluation and mandatory admissions

The [four-perspective reviewer stress test](RITHM_R64_REVIEWER_COURT_20261121.md) is an internally simulated editorial audit, **NOT review by independent humans, another AI, or a journal**. The central objections are (A) novelty collision, (B) nonidentified mechanism due to original info/treatment joint design and researcher post-selection, (C) after-session tuning and conditional-exogenous replay, (D) reproducibility strength not sufficient for novel findings.

R64's required steps BEFORE another new latent model:

1. Reproduce exact original-authors Small Sample model under the actual period-t information permissions. Establish direct benchmark parity with the original source and published outputs.
2. Implement legally constrained EWA, Q-learning or attraction learner, mixed logit with individual effects, payoff-logit/tenure hazard and calibrated independent model; match sample sizes and held-out original sessions.
3. Keep one-step person logloss, joint group U,D, efficient A4 all-stay, generated 40-round congestion/gross switches, treatment contrast and computational cost as SEPARATE objectives. Freeze evaluation rules before further testing.
4. Require independently recruited/collected groups or a genuinely new mathematical identification result for a major contribution. Do not use 8 source-panel resamples as 8 studies.
5. For a new experiment, follow [prospective randomized group-state and information protocol](RITHM_R64_PROSPECTIVE_IDENTIFICATION_DESIGN_20261121.md), including post-treatment A4 selection bias precautions, formal ethics approval, and prospective power calculation.

**If these gates cannot be met:** preserve honest negative findings and submit, if appropriate, a modest replicable benchmark/replication study, not a causal policy paper. Model proliferation without independent discrimination should stop.
