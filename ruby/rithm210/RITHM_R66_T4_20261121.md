### Information filtration and model rivals

For held-out original session s, train baseline probability of actor flipping at t->t+1 on all other sessions only, using original treatment, actor information flag, prior actor route, own route tenure, and **own observed payoff at t** (available after choice t). Previous routes/own payoffs at t+1 or otherwise unobservable choices are forbidden.

Sequentially update this baseline, always **before** revealing current t->t+1 action, from the same actor's own completed switch trials at earlier periods u<t, separately for each prior route. Fixed-latent actor-rival posterior:

`p_static = (10*p_legal + sum_{u<t,route_u=route_t} flip_u)/(10 + N_{u<t,route_u=route_t})`.

Adaptive-recency rival at frozen decay lambda=0.85:

`p_recent=(10*p_legal+sum lambda^(t-1-u)*flip_u)/(10+sum lambda^(t-1-u))`, with same prior-route restriction. lambda=0.5 is an additional stress test; none is chosen on an untouched independent cohort. All personal features are actor-legal; exact group vector and group age are explicitly **analyst-only**.

For an adversarial group-frailty benchmark, predict group A4 all-stay directly from earlier group A4 all-stay events with Beta-like prior strength 8 and the same static/recency alternatives; this is **not** a player cognition model.

## 4. Exact finite-horizon nonidentification counterexample, separate from human causal claim

Suppose stable latent types j have fixed hazard h_j, initial proportions w_j, and one uninterrupted spell beginning at k=0. For each k>=0,

`S(k)=sum_j w_j(1-h_j)^k`;

`H(k)=sum_j w_j h_j(1-h_j)^k/S(k)`.

Let E_k and Var_k denote latent hazard moments among survivors at k. The exact identity is

`H(k)-H(k+1)=Var_k(h)/(1-H(k)) >= 0`, whenever the denominator is positive.

This follows because surviving one further period reweights types by 1-h. More importantly, define a **single evolving-hazard population** with history-dependent hazard H(k) at age k. Its survival probabilities and age-specific exit distributions are EXACTLY identical to those of the original fixed heterogeneous types, by telescoping `S(k+1)=S(k)(1-H(k))`. Frailty-selection and genuine duration-dependent revision can be observationally equivalent for an isolated spell, without long longitudinal individual identity or independent type variation.

The source-free fraction checker uses (w1,w2)=(1/2,1/2), (h1,h2)=(1/10,3/5), verifies the exact analytical monotonicity identity and matches the full survival/exit distributions over 30 periods using rational arithmetic. This is classical duration-model identification logic, **not newly invented**. Recurrent entry into exact A4, observed identities, and condition-changing policy shocks require extra assumptions; this theorem cannot by itself explain the human Full pattern.

Literature (verified metadata): Heckman & Singer (1984), *A Method for Minimizing the Impact of Distributional Assumptions in Econometric Models for Duration Data*, Econometrica 52(2):271–320, https://ideas.repec.org/a/ecm/emetrp/v52y1984i2p271-320.html ; Lancaster (1979), *Econometric Methods for the Duration of Unemployment*, Econometrica 47(4):939–956, https://ideas.repec.org/a/ecm/emetrp/v47y1979i4p939-56.html ; newer relevant decompositions https://academic.oup.com/restud/article/91/6/3151/7462694 .
