## P2-R1a: same-time different-group residual contrast
Define U B→A switches and D A→B; rU = U - E_hat[U] and rD = D - E_hat[D], with heldout actor-level fitted probability sums. Compute mean rU*rD within the same group; negative-control-like contrast multiplies group g rU by the mean rD of *other groups* in the same session, section, period. Evaluate matched within-minus-other on exactly the same 5,148 eligible groups (from 5,616 total; some cells have one group). This is not a randomized negative control and is imperfect for group-specific unobservables.

20,000 whole-session resamples, seed 20261103, 95% exploratory percentile bounds:
- Within-group all 5,616: **+0.0512449295**, CI **[+0.0330603404,+0.0705053283]**.
- Cross-group, matched eligible 5,148: **+0.0134350166**, CI **[-0.0026287784,+0.0309284124]**.
- Paired difference, 5,148: **+0.0349487260**, CI **[+0.0108837149,+0.0577186128]**.
- Treatment No n=1872: within +0.0375786713, CI [+0.0107893381,+0.0664235695].
- Treatment Partial n=1872: +0.0692654494, CI [+0.0387805285,+0.1049063010].
- Treatment Full n=1872: +0.0468906678, CI [+0.0212380316,+0.0767266615].
A treatment difference has NOT been directly tested; ranking these estimates as significant is forbidden. Positive same-group excess is compatible with group-specific latent common shocks, persistent hidden actor traits, miscalibration and strategic interaction. It does NOT identify coordination.

## P2-R1b: previously hidden temporal calibration drift
33,696 individual heldout decisions: overall predicted mean switch 0.2640449271; observed 0.2643933998; observed-predicted bias +0.0003484728; Brier 0.1809573141. The near-zero *overall* bias masks early and late effects:
- No early +0.00127923, late -0.00360911;
- Partial early +0.01600153, late -0.01363821;
- Full early +0.02952720, late -0.02756937.
Early=periods 1–10; middle=11–29; late=30–39. These are descriptive differences, not independently tested significant causal effects.

## P2-R1c: fold-trained epoch-conditioned actor Markov challenge
Add period epoch to actor-state key; train epoch-cell switch frequencies on the same 20 training sessions per fold, hierarchical shrinkage to train-only baseline actor Markov (alpha=20). Never use heldout outcomes in fit.
- Joint U,D logloss: baseline 1.9809803316 vs epoch 1.9769391096, pooled delta **-0.0040412219**.
- Next-A count logloss: 1.3999243859 vs 1.3979152691, delta **-0.0020091168**.
- Residual cross-moment: +0.0512449295 vs +0.0489345638, delta **-0.0023103657**.

20,000 paired whole-session bootstrap (seed 20261103):
- Joint loss delta CI [-0.00958647,+0.00201106], improvement 16/24 sessions.
- Count loss delta CI [-0.00511515,+0.00104930], improvement 12/24 sessions.
- Cross-moment delta CI [-0.00435498,-0.00022349], reduction in 17/24 sessions.
Therefore temporal calibration is a plausible contributor but not a complete explanation; confidence intervals for predictive score improvements include zero and models were developed on the same sample.
