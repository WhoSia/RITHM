## Central question

Does the Full/Endogenous difference in old-A4 stability arise from **actor-visible own payoff and route tenure**, from the **survivor selection of old six-person configurations**, or from genuinely shared revision clocks? These explanations compete: a better predictor of a group event is not automatically a cognitive or causal mechanism.

## Fee definition sensitivity: unexpected Endogenous asymmetry

Source `profit = Value − Cost` holds exactly for every included No/Partial/Full record (11,520 actor-period rows in each condition). In Endogenous, **3,960 of 11,520 actor-period rewards differ** by recorded information bonus/fee, and **491 of 11,520** change the coarse own-payoff bucket. Reconstructing `Value−Cost` as an analyst-only route-reward sensitivity leaves all three exogenous conditions byte-for-byte identical in output and changes Endogenous estimates only modestly:

- Endogenous late legal own-profit actor logloss **.36104**, reconstructed price-excluded reward **.35972**. Difference −.00132, 24-session exploratory 95% [−.00357,+.00072].
- Endogenous late group all-stay Brier **.19884** vs **.19803**; difference −.00081, 95% [−.00359,+.00145].
- Reconstructed route reward is NOT assumed independently visible; participant-legal mainline uses their actual received `profit` only. Price selection, treatment order and strategic incentives remain confounded.

**Interpretation:** Fee mixing is a real measurement/construct trap, but the coarse-binned fee correction alone does not eliminate the late Endogenous group mismatch. Do NOT use this as a causal estimate of information fees.

## Pure-mathematical alternative to psychological duration dependence

Even without changing **any individual's or group's update rule**, selection among heterogeneously persistent configurations can yield declining exit hazard with age. Let latent type j have fixed exit hazard h_j and prior weight w_j. After survival k−1 transitions, posterior π_k(j) is proportional to w_j(1−h_j)^(k−1). Then exactly,

`h̄(k+1) − h̄(k) = − Var_{π_k}(h) / (1−h̄(k)) ≤ 0.`

For two equally common constant-hazard types 0.10 and 0.60, pooled departure hazards are 0.3500 at age 1, 0.25385 at age 2, 0.18247 at age 3 and approximately 0.11878 at age 5. No underlying within-type cognition changes. Fully rational exact-fraction synthetic tests reproduce this. **This is classical frailty selection, not a novel RITHM theorem.** See Heckman & Singer (1984) [RePEc](https://ideas.repec.org/a/ecm/emetrp/v52y1984i2p271-320.html) and Lancaster (1979) [RePEc](https://ideas.repec.org/a/ecm/emetrp/v47y1979i4p939-56.html). Their duration/selection frameworks do not by themselves validate this specific six-agent congestion mechanism.
