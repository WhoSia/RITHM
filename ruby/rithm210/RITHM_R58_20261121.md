# RITHM-2.10 · P3-R58 — Information-Lawful Learning Rivals, Session-Heldout Forecasts & Cost-Score Separation

**2026-11-21 KST · original-author-approved 24-session retrospective audit · no new human experiment.**

## 1. Research question and decision

R57 showed that a person's previous route tenure, and more tentatively their previously **received** profits, predict the next observed simultaneous group update better than the R56 analyst-occupancy benchmark. R58 asks whether the original experiment's theoretically motivated, rule-level learning models explain those heldout decisions as well as a simple history-based predictive model. It also measures how prediction errors for **actual simultaneous group congestion and number of individual switches** combine under an unmeasured switch-resource price κ. Neither exercise identifies cognitive causation, welfare treatment effects, or interpersonal coordination.

**Result:** On all **24 leave-one-session-out folds**, a previous private-route/tenure/received-payoff/informed-mask benchmark without group occupancy scored **1.980310 nats** per joint (B→A,A→B) update, versus **2.081873 EWA-lite**, **2.502509 SSM1–9**, **2.780266 SSM5** and **2.638928 training-selected SSMq**, under the explicitly augmented SSM 2% lapse rule. R57's stronger analyst-occupancy plus tenure+profit benchmark scored **1.951751**. The legal private benchmark's positive predictive advantage over EWA-lite is visible at the level of exact heldout sample arithmetic; it does not establish why any individual persisted or changed routes.

## 3. Exact predictor information filtration

Every heldout prediction at period t is constructed **before** reading any row's period-t choices or payoff. Following a decision, historical `RouteA`, own `profit`, and—if the participant was informed—the previous group's congestion outcomes may enter the state for t+1. Under **No information**, a participant does not observe the analyst's exact prior group occupancy. The two newly added *participant-lawful* discriminative benchmarks use only the actor's own previous route, own observed consecutive-route tenure, their own received profit, treatment label and informed flag. Neither includes analyst-observed group `NumA`.

The old R57 `state_independent` 1.999614 and `tenure_profit` 1.951751 do include actual previously observed occupancy (an **analyst oracle** for No-information), retained only to allow byte-exact numerical cross-reference. Comparing any cognitive rule directly to that oracle alone is not a fair information-lawful model comparison.

**EWA-lite Appendix A reconstruction:** start at `N(0)=1`, attractions `A_A(0)=A_B(0)=1`; after t use the observed route and *received profit*, not a future stage outcome. Reinforce the chosen action and only reinforce the unchosen action if its exact (informed) or Tables 2–3 belief-weighted (uninformed) foregone profit is no less than actual earnings. Update change detector `phi=1-0.5 Σ_k [h_t(k)-r_t(k)]²`, then `N_t=phi*N_{t-1}+1`, attractions by Appendix equation (2), and predict the next action by two-action logit. For uninformed, past privately received payoff, not the database's real other-player count, determines their distribution over `k=0,...,5`. Source values of 6 and 16 occur at exact boundaries; the source-free tests verify the tables' `profit=14.0` B endpoint and other boundary cases. **Only λ** is fitted, separately per information treatment by exact one-step Bernoulli likelihood on the other **23 sessions**, restricted to λ∈[0,3]; optimizer golden section with stable logaddexp. This is **not** a re-estimation of the author's Table 6 criterion, and no current heldout outcome is fitted.

**SSM5 Appendix B reconstruction:** informed subjects sample 5 previous periods *with replacement*, comparing matched counterfactual payoffs; the private realized value cancels in A-minus-B difference `11-3k`, where k is the preceding trial's other A-user count. Uninformed subjects sample 5 remembered **actually earned** A payoffs and 5 actually earned B payoffs independently, if each route was tried; otherwise they toss a fair coin. Sampling outcomes are integrated **exactly**, including ties, using finite integer-tenth reward support and FFT convolution; a source-free direct enumeration verifies q=1..4. A participant cannot use another route's unreceived profit under Uninformed.

**SSM1–9 Appendix B reconstruction:** latent q is fixed to the person throughout their 40-period segment, with an initially uniform prior on q∈{1,..,9}; R58 updates the q posterior from that person's **preceding** observed choices and then averages `P(A|q)` before the next decision. This is the Bayesian one-step predictive of the fixed-q mixture, not an author's simulated 40-period joint history. The secondary **SSMq-CV** chooses a single q∈1..9 using only the other 23 sessions' *actor-level* logloss, then scores the excluded session. This does **not** reproduce the authors' normalized-squared-distance tuning criterion.

**Explicit SSM lapse:** Pure best-sample rules can assign probability 0 to a historically observed individual choice. In fact, R58 found **613 such individual predictions out of 33,696** under raw SSM5; individual logloss would then be infinite. For finite proper scoring, R58 declares a *non-author* lapse epsilon `P_lapse(A)=(1-epsilon)P_SSM(A)+epsilon/2` with epsilon=0.02 before evaluation; sensitivity reruns at epsilon=0.005 and 0.05. Never describe lapsed SSM scores as the unmodified author's model.

## 4. Heldout original-source comparison

Each treatment has 1,872 group transitions. Total: **5,616 joint directional update targets**, **33,696 individual choice targets** across 24 heldout sessions. All 40 original periods enter past-history updates but **period 1 is not a scored transition**. Each fold excludes an entire session from *all* parameter fitting, baseline rates, and q selection. Historical choices within a heldout session enter only the actor's **past**, as required for rolling one-step forecasting. Outcomes at period t become training history only after the t forecast has been scored. Group logloss integrates independent actor choices to the joint (U,D) law by exact six-Bernoulli convolution.

## 5. From one-step microbehavior to observed *period-end* macro-cost forecasts

To avoid R54's prohibited invented microaction order, R58 derives from the **simultaneous** six-person Bernoulli law only the observable next-period total A count, the gross number of changed individual routes, and period-end excess congestion `3*(nA-4)^2`. Per-period resource cost has the symbolic form `W_t(κ)=3*(nA_t-4)^2+κ G_t` for an **externally unknown κ≥0**. Let forecasting residuals be `e_C=E_t[excess]-observed excess` and `e_G=E_t[gross]-observed gross`. Then the empirically scored quadratic is exact:

`MSE_m(κ) = A_m + B_m κ + D_m κ²`, with `A_m=mean[e_C²]`, `B_m=2 mean[e_C e_G]`, `D_m=mean[e_G²]`.

| Model | A (κ⁰) | B (κ¹) | D (κ²) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| **Legal private history** | **18.7378** | **1.9766** | **1.1664** |
| EWA-lite | 20.4922 | 3.0159 | 1.3388 |
| SSM1–9 | 38.9650 | 7.1335 | 1.7852 |
| SSM5 | 41.6329 | 7.5856 | 1.8892 |
| SSMq trained | 39.6016 | 8.3864 | 2.0713 |
| R57 oracle + tenure+profit | 18.3844 | 1.9664 | 1.1240 |

For each named Appendix rival, every coefficient of `MSE_rival(κ) − MSE_legal_history(κ)` is **positive in this finite observed cohort**, so the R58 private-history predictor has **strictly smaller *forecast MSE*** for every nonnegative κ. At κ=0/1/5, its MSE advantage relative to EWA is 1.754 / 2.966 / 11.260 squared score units, with descriptive paired session-resampling bands [1.075,2.426], [1.965,3.946], [7.778,14.413], respectively. An independent source-free test expands squared residuals to prove the coefficients and validates the global half-line quadratic minimum, including a case where end-point checks would falsely suggest dominance.

This is **not an identified welfare advantage** of information policy or a measured switching resource price. It is a ranking of heldout mean-squared *prediction errors* for a symbolically priced observable cost. The inequality is numerically strict on observed sessions but should not be generalized to unobserved populations without a sampling model. EWA/SSM's one-step errors and full autonomous 40-round simulations answer different questions. The estimator does not observe within-period ordering or personalized subjective cognitive burdens.

## 6. Independent audits, method limits and novelty gate

**Reproducibility:** `r58_author_rivals.py` contains the full explicit Appendix rule logic; `r58_matched_cv.py` performs session-separated fitting and one-step prediction; `r58_provenance.py` binds the source ZIP raw SHA and denominators; `run_r58.py` checks the origin before writing aggregate JSON. Tests reject a forged ZIP; test V=16/profit14.0 payoff-boundary classifications; verify EWA N/A initial values; verify future choice mutation cannot alter the current forecast; compare FFT q convolution to direct exhaustive enumeration; check per-route exposure probabilities over all 64 possible six-bit route masks; check the latent fixed-q posterior, private-legal occupancy omission, and symbolic κ-MSE quadratic algebra. **20 source-free tests passed locally**, while the original sealed 24-session replay passed in a separate source-accessible environment. GitHub Actions cannot run the original data in CI and may never certify it.

**Sensitivity:** SSM's lapse is investigator-added, q predictive selection differs from the author's simulation moments, and the EWA MLE is trained by one-step Bernoulli logloss with a chosen λ∈[0,3] bound; individual taste/early practice history, latent common environmental shocks, other proper-scoring rules, model effective capacity and heterogeneity may change conclusions. All 20 preceding practice periods are excluded from the fitted actor attractions/histories, consistent with the Appendix simulations but not necessarily the subjects' subjective memory. Per-treatment, early/late split and multi-score MSE diagnostics are included in the aggregate JSON, **not unreported individual rows**. The original author explicitly discusses carryovers in Appendix E, which is not yet matched in this evaluator.

**Prior-art boundary:** The EWA-lite and Small Sample learning rules and their approximate fit to route-choice patterns are already described by Noussair and Qiao, with underlying EWA models by Ho–Camerer–Chong and Small Sample methods by Erev–Roth. No general learning theory is invented here. R58's contribution is a **documented and reproducible information-lawful one-step rival benchmark, paired with exact symbolic κ macro-forecast error algebra** on the authorized history. Its extension to cognition/coordination *causal identification* remains unresolved.

## 7. Research-director decision: R59

**R59 proposed formal name:** *Prior-Exposure, Latent Heterogeneity & Free-Running Welfare Transport: Matching Appendix Dynamics to Session-Heldout Sequential Evidence*.

The next essential advance is to distinguish **one-step teacher-forced prediction** from a truly **free-running 40-period route-choice simulation**. Fit EWA λ, q/lapse or model selection only on the 23 eligible training sessions, simulate heldout group trajectories using the actual historical feedback filtration, seed and replicate enough runs to audit Monte Carlo uncertainty, then compare **joint actor turnover, period-end congestion and symbolic κ resources** on identical session partitions. Simultaneously compare a participant-private learned hazard, a permanent actor random effect and a group common shock. No full causal mechanism attribution without new identifying information, and **no new human recruitment**.
