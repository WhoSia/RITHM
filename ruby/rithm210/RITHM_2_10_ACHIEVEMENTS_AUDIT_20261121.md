## A. Core question and historical continuity

The **founding problem** was *how individual hassle, inertia, limited information, trust, accumulated experience and adjustment opportunities interact with route choice and generate persistent network congestion, hysteresis and welfare outcomes*. At 2.10's opening (2026-11-03), emphasis shifted to **mechanism identifiability**: Which different latent learning, independent revision, common-shock and strategic coordination models give the same observable choice histories? The 2026-11-04 scope audit explicitly subordinated that necessary identification work to the original **micro friction → individual adaptation → macro welfare** program. RITHM is not now merely an accuracy leaderboard.

### C. Distinguish the five levels of evidence

1. **Exact mathematics** proves identities and counterexamples under stated assumptions; it does **not** identify real-human mechanisms.
2. **Original-source integrity** establishes the sample and reproducibility; 24 sessions, not 34,560 independent population draws.
3. **Out-of-session prediction** measures future observable choices given legal prior histories; it does **not** identify effects of information interventions.
4. **Self-generated macro simulation** tests model transport under chosen initial values/feedback laws; it does **not** guarantee accurate causal policy welfare.
5. **Causal behavioral/collective welfare mechanism** remains **HOLD** until policy-relevant potential outcomes or credible experimental variation distinguish hidden traits, accumulated learning, simultaneous coordination and price-bearing actual switching.
