## 1. An epistemic principle rather than a novelty veto

A model or paper can be partly right and partly wrong. Rejected interpretations and unsuccessful mechanisms should remain **versioned hypotheses**, not be deleted from the research process. Evidence requirements are specific to a claim: exploratory thought and counterexample construction require no artificial p-value gate; descriptive data reports require exact provenance; predicting a new regime demands an explicit benchmark; claims about actual psychological or causal mechanisms demand genuinely discriminating observations. Failure at the latter level does not prohibit continued exploration of the idea.

References are **arguments to confront and modify**, not authorities whose presence automatically cancels originality. In particular, original SSM, EWA-lite, reinforcement-learning, inertia, group-shock and individual-feedback accounts remain coequal, contestable competitors. RITHM should invent a hypothesis, confront it with its strongest alternative, preserve a counterexample, refine it, then ask what new phenomenon the refined hypothesis predicts. The question is what the data or mathematics **rules out**, **cannot distinguish**, and **leaves open**. Avoid both cherry-picking and endless threshold-based suppression of speculative ideas.

## 2. R65A — A less-used fourth regime in the authorized original data

Original paper Sections 4.1–4.2 describe four 40-period conditions. No, Partial and Full were ordered and repeated within the same participant groups in Part 1. **Part 2 Endogenous** was always last; people expressed whether they would access congestion information under incentive-dependent terms. This regime is neither an independent experiment nor simply another level of randomized information.

Within each treatment/period, actors are matched in memory. No actor ID, individual record or group trajectory is serialized. The same 48 groups are reused across all four conditions.

## 3. R65B — The original authors' fitted, not only illustrative, Full SSM

The original author's [online Appendix B.1 Table 4 and D Table 6(b)](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1xwIj82h_LTrgffv2w4XtJs2KDc8980RW/view) explicitly distinguish a *sample of q* model, and *sample of 1 to q* assigned as permanent player type. In the Full condition and Segments 1–3, **Full-specific fitted q = 3 for SSMq and qmax = 4 for SSM1-q**. SSM5 q5 is the illustration already reproduced at R7; the fitted parameters were absent from R64's parity agenda.

An independent source-free Python simulator implements the same Full six-person simultaneous rules. If \(a_{-i}(t)\) is the number of *other* people on A, the experienced hypothetical payoff advantage for informed player i is

\[
\pi_i(A,t)-\pi_i(B,t)=11-3a_{-i}(t).
\]

The private value cancels because it is route-independent and unknowable pre-choice. For each next choice, a player samples q own historical observations **with replacement** and chooses the larger average payoff, random choice on ties. No original human records or researcher future observation enter this simulation.

## 4. R65C — A surprising ambiguity in the original late-stability anomaly

Study the age \(\ell\) of the SAME six-person route-assignment vector at time t (consecutive periods without any person's route change), conditional on previous A4. A4 occupancy stability by itself is NOT equal to exact unchanged route-label configuration; balanced U=D>0 swaps preserve occupancy while changing persons' routes. Different aging clocks imply different hypotheses.

Full original one-period-old configurations: early all-stay **20/122=0.16393**, late **36/108=0.33333**. Longer joint configuration ages become more frequent late. One symmetric Kitagawa decomposition of the Full late−early total +0.36288 using the four age bins 1,2,3–4,5+ gives:

- Changing weights of age strata **+0.22863**; within-age-bin conditional probability differences **+0.13425**.
- But a legitimate **two-bin alternative**, age 1 vs age ≥2, assigns weights/composition **+0.13118**, within-bin change **+0.23170**.
- Both sum to the SAME +0.36288. The 'composition fraction' can shift from about 63% to about 36% merely by regrouping ages, so **it is NOT a uniquely identified explanation**.
- The two-bin 24-session bootstrap, 3,000 draws: composition descriptive95 **[+0.05670,+0.18993]**; within-bin descriptive95 **[+0.12445,+0.37334]**; total95 **[+0.21955,+0.48649]**.
- Endogenous four-bin composition +0.03144 and within-bin −0.03979, net −0.00836; under two bins +0.00701 and −0.01537. Two-bin Endogenous composition and within intervals include zero.

This is arithmetic decomposition, not psychological proof. Conditioning on A4 and episode age involves prior individual/group outcomes; information treatments and group histories determine who enters each age stratum. Four-bin early Full age≥5 had only four observations. The source-free test explicitly refuses to invent a rate for an unobserved bin; synthetic tests initially failed because one fixture had no late age-1 rows, and they were repaired to treat it as **undefined**, not silently fill zero.

**Hypothesis D1:** late stability arises through selective prevalence of longer-lived route configurations even under a stationary individual hazard. **Competing D2:** within-episode switching propensity changes with experience, perceived benefit, group expectation or private risk. **D3:** both mechanisms operate; bin counts themselves are arbitrary discretizations of a more informative continuous duration-dependent process. Keep all three active.

## 5. The object of the next experiment is NOT 'beat paper X' by .001 loss

Prior R63B/R64 one-step and 40-step reversals remain worth preserving, but they are not a final research purpose. Three distinctions should organize RITHM's next step:

1. **Feasible cognition:** distinguish the individual's personally available information from modeler-only six-person states. An informed Full participant may see previous complete congestion; an uninformed No participant cannot. Avoid assuming *all* actors have either information.
2. **Two clocks and two conservation laws:** the person has experience time and route tenure; the group has an exact configuration-duration clock and an occupancy-duration clock. Gross turnover (U+D) differs from net congestion migration (U−D). A claim about stable occupancy need not imply stable people or route commitments.
3. **Unanticipated endogenous regime:** forced Full and voluntarily self-selected Endogenous are neither identical treatments nor independent cohorts. That very non-equivalence is an empirical opponent to simplistic 'more information always stabilizes groups' stories, not an excuse to discard the Endogenous data.

Confront a variety of mechanisms on exact, falsifiable target moments (early/late Full A4 all-stay, age-stratified hazard, balanced compensation, all-information-voluntary Endogenous, and generated 40-round group costs). Reject the **claim** contradicted by evidence, not the general research idea; retain negative and alternative results for future revisions.
