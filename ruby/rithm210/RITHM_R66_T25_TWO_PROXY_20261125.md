# RITHM R66-T25 — Switch-Anchored Two-Proxy Identification and Local Dependence
2026-11-25 KST. Conditional latent-class construction, not a measured human cognitive gate.
## A. Identification
E latent reconsideration, S observed own switch, S=1 implies E=1. Assume S independent of joint two binary tags Z1,Z2 conditional on E, tags independent conditional on E. s=P(S=1)>0. A=P(Z1,Z2|S=1) known; R unconditional tag table. R=q A+(1-q)B where B is inactive tag distribution. For informative nondegenerate tags, alpha_i=P(Z_i=1|S=1), m_i=P(Z_i=1), C=Cov(Z1,Z2), q=C/[C+(alpha1-m1)(alpha2-m2)]. Equivalent det(R-qA)=det(R)-q*[R00*A11+R11*A00-R01*A10-R10*A01]=0. Check nonnegative B, s<=q<=1 and exact independence; p=s/q. When cross coefficient and det(R) both zero, identify only q in [s,min(1,min_z R_z/A_z)] over positive A_z, with q=1 allowed iff R=A. No switch anchor at s=0; reject. Labels fixed by switch anchor, not arbitrary class naming.
