## Micro→macro mathematical target

Let A occupancy before revision be four, with simultaneous A→B decisions `D` and B→A decisions `U`. The next-round excess congestion cost is **`L=3(U-D)^2`**. Exactly,

`E[L]/3 = Var(U) + Var(D) - 2 Cov(U,D) + (E[U]-E[D])^2`.

This is a classical second-moment identity, not a novel theorem. Crucially, gross revisions `U+D`, directional covariance and net occupancy changes are different economic objects. Opposite-direction synchronized swaps can reduce `L`, while synchronized same-direction moves may increase it.

Parallel Work obtained, as supplied by the user, a rational one-clock comparison for `a` eligible A→B and `b` eligible B→A agents: conditional on random shared hazard `R`, each eligible actor changes independently; compare to completely independent changes with the same individual expected hazard `E[R]`. Exact difference: `Δ E[L]=3 Var(R)[(b-a)^2-(a+b)]`. User-reported examples at `nA=4` and `R∈{0,1}` each with probability 1/2: `(a,b)=(4,0)` adds `+9`; `(4,2)` adds `−1.5`. We verified the formula algebraically independently; Work's Windows-local code remains external to this chat and its stated three passing tests are not GitHub CI results. This one-period identity does NOT specify a long-run welfare comparison.

## Full early→late cost identity: what changed?

Exactly decompose `Δ E[L] = 3Δ Var(U) + 3Δ Var(D) − 6Δ Cov(U,D) + 3Δ(E U−E D)^2`.

For Full, total **−1.688741** cost units are accounted for by:

- B→A directional count variance change **−0.68837**;
- A→B directional count variance change **−0.77703**;
- the opposite-direction covariance term **+0.00764** (essentially unchanged across observed early/late groups);
- mean signed net-change term **−0.23099**.

Thus the observed drop mainly coincides with both **directional revision variances shrinking**, not a large early/late change in the aggregate Cov(U,D) itself. This is an exact descriptive moment partition, NOT an identified decomposition of causes. Individual probabilities, information, own tenure, and which A4 risk-set states survive can all change the aggregate terms.

## Competitor baselines and falsification

Each of 24 original sessions is held out completely. For each target condition, train individual flip propensities on **the other 23 sessions only** and assume Bernoulli independence conditional on the listed predictors. Apply the resulting Bernoulli means/variances to each observed A4 risk set.

- `legal_coarse`: own prior route, own info flag, treatment context and epoch; train on all source occupancy states, not using actual old occupancy as a human feature.
- `legal_tenure`: same plus **own route streak tenure** bins `1/2/3–4/5+`; 20-count hierarchical shrinkage to the above stratum, trained on other-session observations.
- `analyst_A4`: **investigator-only** old A4 conditional propensity trained on other original sessions, shrunk toward legal coarse. This uses group occupancy unseen by originally uninformed actors and is NEVER attributed to their cognition.

The authoritative JSON supplies full-precision values for all reported means. `Observed − predicted` Full late welfare residual for `legal_tenure` is about **−0.8292**, while `analyst_A4` shrinks it to about **−0.2776**, with 24-original-session descriptive bootstrap 95% **[−0.690,+0.259]** (3,000 resamples). Thus the investigator-only marginal recalibration explains much of the discrepancy and remaining difference is not distinguishable from zero on this repeated cohort under this descriptive interval. It does NOT prove independence, nor rule out a shared clock.

A second caution: for Endogenous late, analyst-only residual remains approximately **−0.813**, with descriptive 95% **[−1.135,−0.419]**, even after partial old-A4 correction. This may reflect richer actor heterogeneity, shared exposure and price-driven information selection, unobserved state or dependence. It is *not* an identified common clock or causal effect. This should be studied as a competing mechanism in R66-T3, not normalized away.

`Actor logloss`, `all-six-stay Brier`, `E[L]`, directional moments and independent fitted propensities are separate evaluation objects. Neither a good welfare mean nor a good one-step individual likelihood establishes a mechanism.

## New falsifiers and next research

1. **MARGINAL-P0**: If correctly calibrated own-history marginals under observational support explain A4 gross-change and net-second-moment errors, a novel collective timing state is unnecessary on that subset.
2. **COMMON-CLOCK**: If cross-fitted lawful individual models remain calibrated but observed *joint* directional moments consistently disagree beyond session-cluster uncertainty, test stable shared signals; still distinguish common shock, persistent individual types, endogenous information and strategic reaction.
3. **AGE-SELECTION**: Restrict to comparable own-route tenure and old joint-configuration duration; check how much apparent group dependence is due to old-A4 risk-set age. Note old configuration age is analyst-only for uninformed participants.
4. **ENDOGENOUS**: Do not compare voluntary information arms as randomized; use an explicit selection mechanism and the fixed Part 2 order. The Endogenous late residual survives the simple oracle, requiring wider rivals.
5. **DYNAMIC**: One-step signed-covariance sign need not persist over 40-period self-generated state distributions. Validate any extended claim by a fresh endogenous-state Markov rollout with the same after-choice feedback permissions.
