## 2. Exact No-source-equivalent, Full-target-divergent lawful counterexample

Construct six simultaneous choosers: four always A, one always B, and one flexible actor. If the flexible actor is on B, occupancy is A4; if on A, occupancy is A5. At A4 that actor switches B→A with probability 1/10. Under RESET, the A5 actor switches A→B with probability 1 in both No and Full. Under TRAP, the A5 actor still returns with probability 1 in No, but NEVER returns in Full. The other five do not switch. The flexible actor sees only their own previous route and the announced treatment: neither a researcher-only occupancy nor future reward is an input. This is an illustrative synthetic policy class, NOT a claim about actual No/Full human participants.

At No the two worlds have IDENTICAL conditional transition laws at ALL states and horizons. At Full they are identical for the first two period costs but separate after entry into A5. With cost C(n)=3(n−4)^2, C(A4)=0 and C(A5)=3. Starting in A4 and writing p_t=P(A5 at t), p_1=0:

- RESET: p_(t+1)=(1−p_t)/10.
- TRAP in Full: p_(t+1)=p_t+(1−p_t)/10.
- H=40 exact Fraction DP: RESET E cumulative cost=10.661157024793388; TRAP=90.44342648824303; gap=79.78226946344965.
- Expected total gross moves: RESET=7.198347107438017, TRAP=0.9852191170585654. Thus fewer moves can accompany far worse congestion.

Even perfect calibration on source No trajectories alone therefore cannot certify transport to target Full without a cross-treatment invariance or overlap assumption. This is a standard support/transport obstruction, not a novel theorem and not an estimated intervention effect.

## 3. Classical finite-horizon stability certificate and its premise

For two evolving fully augmented-state kernels K_t,L_t, common initial distribution, common c(x) in [0,M], and SUP over all reachable states and times TV(K_t(x,.),L_t(x,.))≤epsilon, a telescoping/coupling argument gives absolute expected H-period total-cost difference ≤ M Σ_(j=0)^(H−1) min(1,j epsilon). Here M=48 for all six-person occupancy states. This is a conservative standard bound. A teacher-forced logloss or source-only support bound is NOT the premise: the exact counterexample does not satisfy uniform cross-treatment kernel closeness.

Prior art: [Ross, Gordon & Bagnell 2011, PMLR](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v15/ross11a.html) (sequence-induced distribution shift); RITHM R59's earlier generated-state counterexample; original Noussair–Qiao Appendix A/B/E. No novelty claim for the coupling inequality or the transport impossibility pattern.
