## B. Exact pair-moment dynamic ambiguity
T24/T31 absorbing n=5 witness: five A actors may switch, B stays, after any departure from n5 all states absorb. Let M count switches, q its six masses, common individual probability p and common pair intersection t. Exchangeable labelled law obtained by uniform subsets conditional on M, so E[M]=5p and E[M(M-1)]=20t. Feasible count laws form a polytope with nonnegative q, sum1, mean5p, and lower/upper pair moment. All vertices supported on <=2 counts if moment inequality slack, <=3 if one moment bound active (degenerate vertices included). Enumerate using exact rational Lagrange weights: for support {i,j,k}, q_i=(E[M^2]-(j+k)E[M]+jk)/((i-j)(i-k)). Nonnegative and moment-feasible only.
