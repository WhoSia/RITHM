## T21-A clean recommendation
At n=5 select one of five old-A uniformly. Selected actor receives move recommendation with probability q; others never receive it. Receipt itself reveals own selection, silence pools not-selected and selected-but-undelivered. Assume common opportunity and B stays. If everyone obeys, receiver gain=1-k; silent A probability another A received=4q/(5-q), gain=1-k-12q/(5-q); B gain=-4-k+3q. For 0<=k<=1, obedience (weak) iff q>=5(1-k)/(13-k). At n=4 no move messages and all stay is a stage equilibrium. With clean receipt and selection of the obedient branch, p=1/2 common opportunity and H40, exact expected congestion 3*sum_{t=0}^{39}(1-q/2)^t. At q=1 ~6 vs 120 trapped; at k=0 and q=5/13 15.596959. This is constructive bypass of T20 independent equilibrium obstruction by adding a correlated signal, not uniqueness or full repeated-game implementation.
## T21-B false-positive attack
Selected receives with q; each unselected A independently gets false signal with f conditional on selected label. Expected other signals conditional on receipt is 4f+4f(q-f)/(q+4f); conditional silence is 4f+4(1-f)(q-f)/(5-q-4f). Ignore zero-mass signals. Obedience iff receiver gain 1-k-3E[D_other|receipt]>=0, silent gain<=0 and B gain=-4-k+3(q+4f)<=0. At q=1,k=0 receiver requires 36f^2+20f-1<=0, f<=(sqrt(34)-5)/18 ~.04616; f=.05 receiver gain -3/40. q=.5,f=.05 receiver gain 1/70 and obedience holds; q=.25,f=0 fails silent obedience. False-positive calculation is one-period only (overshoot states not assigned dynamic policy). With f=0, B-stays rival branch: received A moves, silent A mixes r=max(0,(1-k-3Q)/(3(4-Q))) with Q=4q/(5-q). Valid branch (not unique); low q triggers spontaneous revision. Real switching resource c distinct, clean horizon expected total includes c*(1-(1-q/2)^(H-1)).
