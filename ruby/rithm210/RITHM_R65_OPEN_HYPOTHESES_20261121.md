# RITHM-2.10 R65 — Open Hypotheses, Adversarial Revisions and Epistemic Pluralism

2026-11-21. A scientific claim may fail while the underlying hypothesis remains useful. Previous literature is an active opponent and source of ideas, not unquestionable authority. This register is NOT a novelty veto or journal judgment. Preserve both negative and positive evidence, failed tests and renewed proposals.

## How evidence should operate

- An exploratory mechanism needs no high p-value threshold to be conceived or simulated. It must not be called confirmed simply because it is conceivable.
- A synthetic proof tests assumptions; original-source descriptive patterns test a specific population; a causal claim needs a distinguishing design. They answer different questions. None automatically disqualifies the others.
- Challenge the scope of the claim, not the intellectual legitimacy of the hypothesis. A counterexample to all SSMs is different from a counterexample to SSM5 at fixed q.
- When an alternative fails, record the exact failure, preserve it as an immutable earlier version and revise the alternative. Do not delete merely because a recent paper is incompatible.
- Use GitHub for scripts, scientific rebuttal and tests, Notion for semantic decisions, and Drive for exact source and reproducer bytes. No GitHub Actions bot author or committer.

## H1. Legally available personal learning

Mechanism: previous own payoff, own route tenure and own switching history can cause or mimic stable behavior without a six-person cognitive oracle. R62 and R63 reduced 40-period macro errors by adding lawful feedback. Rival observation: R63C original A4 all-stay remains underpredicted; an oracle marginal shift improves the no-switch prediction but worsens the entire joint U,D logloss. Next test: a frozen history-legal individual learner with a continuous tenure hazard against fitted original SSM and Full late A4, without illegal group occupancy input for uninformed participants. Status OPEN, not identified.

## H2. Selective survival of joint configurations

Mechanism: at later periods old, long-lived six-person route configurations disproportionately occupy the stable A4 risk set even without a changing individual learning rule. R65C Full four-bin composition 0.22863 and within-bin 0.13425; a two-bin alternative assigns 0.13118 and 0.23170. Both sum to +0.36288, showing that a 63% share is not unique or causal. First-age Full all-stay early 20/122 versus late 36/108 is a rival to a pure composition story. Next test: continuous-age survival models with competing risks; compare exact route-configuration age and occupancy age. Status OPEN.

## H3. Public feedback creates common expectations

Mechanism: previous shared congestion feedback allows aligned beliefs and stable assignments even without explicit coordination. Full old-A4 all-stay grows 0.22078 to 0.58366. Opponent: voluntarily informed Endogenous grows 0.37443 to 0.36607, with different order, selection and incentives. Next test: an actor-legal information/belief updating rule, potentially independent new group-level experiment. Status OPEN, not causal.

## H4. Endogenous selection, price and treatment order

Mechanism: Part2 differences may stem from self-selected information and having experienced three earlier conditions. In Endogenous the 48 group counts by number informed 0/1/2/3/4/5/6 are 17/12/2/3/0/2/12. The original study states Endogenous was always last and employed an incentive-linked strategy method. Opponent: same participants, no independent randomized manipulation of voluntary access or treatment order. Next test: descriptive subgroup comparisons with all-six-informed Endogenous groups and their own earlier Full condition, explicitly retaining selection bias. Status OPEN.

## H5. SSM is correct for its stated target, insufficient for the chosen new event

The original author SSM fit targets average congestion and route A volume. R7 matched Table7 means and SDs while SSM5 missed late Full conditional A4 stability. R65B independently tested author-fitted Full SSMq3 and SSM1-q4: both decrease A4 all-stay from early to late in two 5000-group source-free streams, unlike the original Full cohort. Strong counterpoint: R7 q30 can increase A4 persistence, although its early level is much too high. Next test: joint competing-objective frontier including authors' original score, A4 age-conditioned staying and group turnover. Status OPEN for the model family, a specific selected-moment mismatch for fitted q.

## H6. Genuine shared shock, attention clock or strategic interaction

Mechanism: after calibrating individual marginals, a group-level shared state or interaction creates extra all-six staying. Opponent: R62 joint-logloss selects pause q=0 in all 24 folds; R63C shows individual miscalibration and broad exact Frechet dependence bounds. No causal group coordination demonstrated. Next test: construct observable predictions that cannot be explained by an explicitly fixed legal individual law, using a prospective group-level manipulation if necessary. Status OPEN as a rival explanation, not promoted.

## R65 research pivots and counterexamples not to erase

2. Full shows large observed late-stability rise, Endogenous does not. It is a genuine descriptive challenge to simplistic information-level theories, not a policy-effect estimate.
3. Four-bin and two-bin age decomposition change attribution drastically while preserving the total. No privileged temporal clock is yet known.
4. R63B/R64 one-step versus 40-period model rankings differ. No simplistic pooled-metric hierarchy.
