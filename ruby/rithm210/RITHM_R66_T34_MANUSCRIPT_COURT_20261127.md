## 4. Candidate manuscript (not a claim of firstness)
Working title: From Revision Frictions to Collective Welfare: Identification Across Observation and Time Horizons in Repeated Congestion Choice.
Central question: what observable restrictions suffice to determine the sign of policy-induced cumulative congestion cost when individual decisions are repeatedly coupled by endogenous feedback?
Theorem A: characterize observation fibers and the additional measurements needed to constrain latent revision opportunity vs response (T22–T26; prior latent-class/dynamic-counterfactual literature).
Theorem B: within a specified policy response set, distinguish one-step quadratic moment sufficiency from horizon-specific higher-order requirements; use T7 as algebra, T32/T33 as examples, seek general state-specific necessity/sufficiency for transition value directions and fixed-law versus rectangular sets. Need prove beyond reset and avoid rebranding standard LP/linear algebra. T24/T32/T33 connection provisional N=.60, S=.98, V=.60.
