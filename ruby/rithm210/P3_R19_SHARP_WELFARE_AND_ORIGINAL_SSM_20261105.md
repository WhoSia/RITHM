## R19A–C: sharp welfare identification in a labelled six-agent system
Use exact Rational 64×64 actor-specific transition DP and congestion cost C(n)=n²+2(6−n)². Aggregated observation n=3 identifies baseline 40-period cost 1080 under the identity transition. Under policy that toggles only actor0 each round, the same aggregate baseline trajectory admits J40=1020 or1260, hence sharp welfare-effect range **[-60,180]** from unknown initial composition. These are known stipulated actor laws; **not** information-equivalent with observed complete human actor trajectories.

More informative: for the deterministic one-actor-toggle subclass, given n in1..5 and horizon H, sharp welfare width = **12 floor(H/2)|n−4|**. At n=4,H=40 welfare is **uniquely 1020** despite unidentified actor0 membership. This distinguishes microstate identification from functional welfare identification in an exact restricted case; it is not yet a novel general theorem. A 90%-accurate noisy signal of actor0 route yields value-of-information **18 cost units**, with symmetric prior and Bayes-optimal toggle/no-toggle.

## R19D–F: actual original small-sample learning competition
Revisited author [Online Appendix B.1](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1xwIj82h_LTrgffv2w4XtJs2KDc8980RW/view): informed players sample with replacement from paired, counterfactual-known past route payoffs; uninformed sample route-specific **actually observed** own payoffs, choosing randomly if an action has no past experience. Implemented exact integer-count convolution with Rational choice probabilities, a stated fair-tie convention, and a strict filtration gate excluding hidden NumA for uninformed agents. [Exact SSMq core](p3/r19_ssm_exact.rb), [24-session SHA-verified source scorer](p3/r19_ssm_source_benchmark.rb), [paired session bootstrap](p3/r19_ssm_session_contrast.rb), all source-free regression tested.

From author-approved CSV SHA256 `d36520564b60ba5281e7b571d2ecfd94e055b9ab7f78bd4e9eaf762844d0f8bb`: **24 sessions, 34,560 person-periods, 40 periods, treatments 1–3**. The within-section (restart) exact SSMq models q=1,2,3 are scored on **individual route choice Brier** against Beta(1,1) smoothed prior own-route frequency, not authors' full EWA/SSM1-q MNSD or 40-period generative welfare. Representative Full-informed Briers: own-route **0.173822**; SSM1 **0.217808**; SSM2 **0.203514**; SSM3 **0.201729**.
