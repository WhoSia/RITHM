## Exact result

Let x[t+1]=rho*x[t]+eps*z[t] and J_H=E sum(x[t]^2), x[0]=0, where z[t] is fair +/-1 every period. Compare IID shocks with one shared random sign S used at all dates, or an alternating sign (-1)^t*S. Their per-period marginals are identical.

For H=40, eps=1/5:
- rho=+1/2: IID J=2.06222222; common J=5.97333333 (amplifies).
- rho=0: both J=1.56 exactly (no temporal transmission).
- rho=-1/2: IID J=2.06222222; common J=0.71111111 (attenuates), whereas alternating shared sign J=5.97333333 (amplifies).

The exact finite-horizon identity is
J_H=eps² sum_{t=1}^{H-1} sum_{s,r<t} rho^(2t-2-s-r) E[z_s z_r].
If competing laws have identical diagonal moments and every off-diagonal E[z_s z_r] differs by at most delta, the absolute J-difference is at most 2 eps² delta sum_{t=2}^{H-1} sum_{0<=s<r<t}|rho|^(2t-2-s-r). Exact Fraction source tests check positive/negative sign cases and sharpness for the selected schedules. This is a basic quadratic-form identity, not a novel mathematical theorem and NOT a fitted finite six-person route model.
