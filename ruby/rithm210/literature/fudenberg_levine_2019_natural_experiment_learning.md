# P3-R9 Literature Note F — Fudenberg & Levine (2019 Working Paper)

**Version verified in the actual uploaded PDF:** [Learning in Games and the Interpretation of Natural Experiments](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1QKdUGPGCMh6U4zTKGzEBAtdSESJDRSJu/view). First page explicitly dates this manuscript **2019-11-09**; published American Economic Journal: Microeconomics version is **2022**, but that published version is NOT the same verified Drive byte object. Its filename therefore remains '2019 Author Working Paper'.

## Source theorem and caution
The paper models agents uncertain whether effort effectively affects success, and receiving Bayesian information from success/failure. In its linear-quadratic example, Theorem 2 gives posterior monotonicity and how the informativeness of the success signal varies with effort; Theorem 3 bounds the resulting learning component of choice changes. Theorems 4–5 formally show, under the paper's assumptions, that regression discontinuity or difference-in-differences treatment estimands can mix a **preference effect** with a **learning effect**.

## Consequences for RITHM
Raw Full early→late behavior cannot be interpreted unconditionally as an isolated informational treatment effect or a changed inherent preference. Even at an identical current n_A=4, histories, previous success, opponent beliefs and selection into the conditioning set may differ. In the source route experiment, each observed treatment occurs in sections with its own prior information and participants; the causal intervention is not just a scalar 'informed'.

## Design requirement
Clearly distinguish: (1) observed descriptive epoch contrasts; (2) heldout predictive outcomes; (3) causal effect of an experimentally randomized information assignment conditional on full protocol and carryover; (4) structurally identified counterfactual micro-rules. The current P3-R8 freeze-only no-go concerns a restricted postulated conditional turnover kernel, and is NOT proof that information caused a particular psychological change.
