# P3-R9 Literature Note D — Brock & Hommes (1997) and Related Rational-Route Models

**Primary:** [Brock & Hommes, A Rational Route to Randomness, Econometrica 65(5), 1059–1095](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1KDAC-ljISXVc1zTxYEzVPy7w5Yhk-b2_/view). New 00_INTAKE PDF normalized to 10_PAPERS after original title/metadata check.

## What the original mechanism is
An adaptively rational equilibrium couples endogenous variables with agents selecting among distinct **forecasting rules** by historically measured predictor fitness. Evaluating the more sophisticated rational predictor can itself carry a positive information cost. The paper shows irregular endogenous price dynamics, bifurcations and strange attractors in a cobweb framework under strong sensitivity of predictor selection. Therefore 'small cognitive cost plus rational adaptation causes macroscopic oscillation' was already demonstrated; it is prior art, not RITHM's novel thesis by itself.

## New related papers in 00_INTAKE also normalized
- [Branch & McGough (2008), Replicator Dynamics in a Cobweb Model with Rationally Heterogeneous Expectations, JEBO 65, 224–244](https://drive.google.com/file/d/12_x6fm0_PVQiKXm9nNWskI8D7lXM49mI/view). Generalized replicator-equilibrium dynamics couples belief-rule composition to price; costly rational forecasting can persist while a stable equilibrium excludes strictly dominated predictors.
- [Naimzada & Pireddu (2020), Rational Expectations (May) Lead to Complex Dynamics in a Muthian Cobweb Model with Heterogeneous Agents, JEBO 177, 415–432](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1A0G9CjMqLQMjAC3hIKIrFDzBe-dGpD-w/view). Adding genuinely rational forward-looking producers can enlarge local stability yet enable richer global multistability and chaos: local equilibrium stability is not global welfare stability.
- [Dudek (2010), A Consistent Route to Randomness, JET 145, 354–381](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1pC0LU9YUFHhEmZ-n1udxbyyKdRl23sKi/view). Optimization over costly information/forecasting technologies can induce deterministically chaotic yet statistically self-consistent equilibrium dynamics. Endogenous irregularity is not proof of irrationality or exogenous noise.

## How this threatens and improves RITHM
A proposed 'information processing friction creates congestion volatility' theorem must distinguish itself from these endogenous-expectation models. One meaningful differential prediction could concern **turnover composition U,D, not just mean group occupancy**, under different adjustment clocks and privately observed histories. Novelty requires either a genuinely new identifiable micro-to-welfare mechanism, a sharp theorem in an information-constrained repeated congestion game, or external validation of a previously untested dynamic-welfare implication.
