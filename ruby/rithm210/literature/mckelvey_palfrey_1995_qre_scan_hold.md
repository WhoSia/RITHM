## Established model and what it means
The paper introduces quantal response equilibria, in which each player's choice probability is monotonic in relative expected utility and equilibrium requires **mutually consistent probabilistic strategies**. Existence and logit-equilibrium limiting properties are discussed in the verified first-page abstract; journal metadata DOI 10.1006/game.1995.1023 is corroborated by the official journal listing.

## The model we still need to test
A one-shot logit response with a constant precision parameter does not BY ITSELF imply learning, a change in time-varying information or improved optimal-state persistence over 40 rounds. A dynamic QRE-inspired model must specify how beliefs, precision and information update, and whether each actor may know other players' current actions. Treat a logistic own-route switching rate as a STRONG rival baseline, not evidence of genuine QRE unless the simultaneous fixed-point consistency conditions are satisfied.
