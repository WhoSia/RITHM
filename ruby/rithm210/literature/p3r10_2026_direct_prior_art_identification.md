# P3-R10 Red-Team Literature Audit — Learning, Synchronization, Identifiability, and Short-Horizon Welfare
**Audited 2026-11-05.** Existing Google Drive source originals were read for author metadata, abstract, model assumptions, theorem statements and selected technical arguments. This is NOT a full page-by-page formal proof audit.

## Already in canonical Research OS 10_PAPERS
1. [Yang et al. (2026), Reinforcement Learning-Based Day-to-Day Route Choice Models Under Full and Partial Information Conditions: A Theoretical and Experimental Research](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1MofuqZdbLl_PbArBstOr2TmR0XJvB11c/view). Original paper shows distinct attraction-updating rules in FI and PI, discusses Proposition 1 linking individual stationary choice probabilities and Wardropian expected flows, and uses experiment against EWA, Q-learning, mixed logit, Selten benchmarks. The original PDF is dated **published 2026-08-12**. **Threat:** repeated information-conditioned choice modeling and equilibrium interpretation are not new in RITHM. **Gap to verify:** conditional n=4 gross turnover/cancellation, fixed six-person effect of synchronous adjustment and finite-40-step shock welfare are not established by the inspected theorem excerpt.
2. [Wu, Yin, Lynch & Liu (2026), Statistical Inference of Day-to-Day Traffic Dynamics, arXiv:2605.02806v2](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1LQON5ENZefmWAuMgwy6r9s4xv7GytDsv/view), PDF revision **2026-08-18**. Theorem 1 establishes identification of `eta,theta,rho` **under Assumption 1**; Theorem 2 yields estimator consistency with compact interior parameter, positive continuous prior and **Assumption 2**. Authors explicitly note identification does NOT imply consistency if trajectories cease to provide new information. Their Proposition 1 connects a restricted individual model to aggregate Horowitz dynamics. **Threat:** basic behavioral-parameter identification and uncertain learning across individual traffic trajectories are prior art. **Gap:** this RITHM clock example's `s` is not empirically identified at all; identifying it from actual limited-information data requires separate experiments or additional constraints, rather than citing the theorem.
3. [Qi, Jia, Qu & He (2023), Investigating Day-to-Day Route Choices Based on Multi-Scenario Laboratory Experiments. Part I: Route-Dependent Attraction and Its Modeling](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1r2M3lM2DHKD1MvPE_5iWg489fVzwdZVk/view). Original preprint identifies three switching patterns in **312 subjects across eight scenarios**, route-dependent inertia/preferences and analytical dynamic equilibrium; paper notes even when multiple pure equilibrium *profiles* exist, equilibrium flows may be the same. **Threat:** inertia and learning explaining repeated lab congestion are not novel contributions.
4. [Yu, Han & Ochieng (2020), Day-to-Day Dynamic Traffic Assignment with Imperfect Information, Bounded Rationality and Information Sharing](https://drive.google.com/file/d/14YM_VDMVrxnMZ5DxBBD1VhBO2PpzItdd/view). Combines partial information, boundedly rational indifference bands, information-sharing and dynamic network loading. **Threat:** the exact ideas information constraints and behavioral bands alone do not establish novelty.
5. [Smith et al. (2014 accepted manuscript), The Long Term Behaviour of Day-to-Day Traffic Assignment Models](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1MXELrCL2Bgf4-ZaF_EgPUQZFM57sPRLF/view). Deterministic multi-attractor versus stochastic unique-stationary interpretations and hybrid dynamics. **Threat:** the noncommuting long/short time narrative has an important day-to-day traffic prior art, though this specific quadratic six-actor clock law has not been checked there.

Its exact theorems and pre-publication versions were NOT examined; high priority **POTENTIAL NEAR-DUPLICATE** of generic inertia/synchronization-based stability claims. ## Boundaries that block premature novelty claims
1. `s` is a **protocol parameter**, not a psychological inertia parameter measured by the experiment.
2. At n=4 perfect current-state strict best responses yield no switching for any s; no fixed s can explain 34/154 early real n4 no-switch events or 150/257 late ones. A separately information-feasible belief/learning/error model is necessary.
3. The exact `12/[5(1-s)^2]` leading escape time is a finite synthetic Markov result. Its algebra alone does not establish journal originality without evaluating the near-duplicate papers and their special cases.
4. The observed 24-session descriptive pattern, simulated SSM5 conditional moments, mathematical clock-only afterlife and inferred human psychology are **four distinct evidence types**. Never substitute one for another.
5. A rigorous next P3-R11 paper candidate should specify a testable, individual-information-feasible departure mechanism; a stochastic activation process; map predicted joint U,D and time-distributed welfare; and then compare prospective or heldout groups. Test *identification* of both the belief-update and adjustment parameters, rather than merely minimizing logloss on the same human data.
