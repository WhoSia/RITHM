## 2. Exact rational synthetic welfare

Original 6-player game costs: \(C(n_A)=n_A^2+2(6-n_A)^2=24+3(n_A-4)^2\). At prior A=4, define \(K\in\{0,1,2,3,4\}\) to count **A→B changes only**, excluding simultaneous B→A changes. Adopt a specifically **hypothetical** policy of exactly one B→A correction per subsequent period after \(K\) departures:

\[
F(K)=3\sum_{j=1}^{K}j^2=\frac{K(K+1)(2K+1)}2.
\]

This is not actually observed human 40-period cost. Define \(m_d=E[\binom{K}{d}]/\binom4d\). Then

\[
E[F(K)]=12m_1+54m_2+24m_3.
\]

One-period cost \(E[3K^2]\) only depends on \(m_1,m_2\). At \(m_1=1/2,m_2=1/4\), one-step excess is **15** but exact feasible long-recovery \(E[F]\in[43/2,47/2]=[21.5,23.5]\); adding \(m_3=1/8\) point-identifies **22.5**. Exact Fraction Gauss–Jordan and vertex enumeration produce attainable sharp endpoints, never relying on opaque numerical LP optimality.

## 4. Conservative simultaneous population sampling region

Under independent identically distributed **whole original session** vectors, for each 10-round epoch and treatment, \(0\le A_{d,s}\le B_s\le20\); \(m_d=E[A_{d,s}]/E[B_s]\) among a population of source-like sessions. For \(S=24\), \(M=2\) numerator moments plus one denominator, Bonferroni/union Hoeffding radius

\[
\varepsilon=B_{max}\sqrt{\frac{\log(2(M+1)/\alpha)}{2S}},
\quad B_{max}=20,\ \alpha=.05.
\]

Confidence intervals for both ratio moments propagate via exact rational sharp probability constraints. If the denominator expectation lower bound is zero, the moment bound reverts to [0,1]. For **all eight original treatment×period cells**, this distribution-free 95%-simultaneous procedure yields \(m_1,m_2\in[0,1]\) and the full welfare set \(E[F]\in[0,90]\). This is honestly **uninformative**; the original source does not certify a narrow population claim by this route. A separate original-session cluster bootstrap 1,000-draw descriptive band for Full late F is approximately **[0.703,1.744]**, but must not be advertised as distribution-free simultaneous coverage or independent external confirmation. Cluster IID is itself an assumption, not a logical guarantee.

## 6. Adversarial theorem audit

Yang et al. (2026) \(Mathematics\) 14(16):2910 generic Proposition 3 as printed requires positive definite continuous V and \(\Delta V\le0\) and concludes **uniform asymptotic stability**. As a general statement this is logically insufficient: \(x_{t+1}=x_t,\ V=x^2\) satisfies all listed inequalities but every nonzero trajectory stays forever; similarly \(x_{t+1}=-x_t\). Strict decline or an invariance/detectability argument would be required. **This is a proof-obligation counterexample, not a finding that their specific full-information dynamics diverge or their empirical results are invalid.**
