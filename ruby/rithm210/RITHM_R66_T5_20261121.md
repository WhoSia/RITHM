# RITHM-2.10 P3-R66-T5 — Micro Friction, Correlated Revision Clocks & Finite-Horizon Welfare Sign Reversal

2026-11-21 KST | Source-free exact seven-state six-person Markov analysis | **Synthetic counterexample, NOT causal/empirical confirmation or new general game-theory theorem**.

## 1. Reconnected original research mission

The goal is to understand **microscopic attention, memory, behavior and switching friction -> conditional individual revision -> group-level covariance/adjustment clocks -> long-lived macro congestion and social welfare**. R66-T1/T2 established covariance decomposition on the six-person experiment; T3/T4 compared observed legal payoffs, individual learning, duration selection and revealed that an unobserved fixed frailty can mimic an evolving hazard on a single spell. T5 turns the exact group-covariance expression into an autonomous dynamic welfare calculation instead of optimizing a one-step predictor.

The six-person congestion cost functions ARE those in the original Noussair & Qiao experiment: route A cost per user `c_A=n_A`, route B cost per user `c_B=2n_B`; hence total cost `C(n)=n²+2(6-n)²=24+3(n-4)²`. We report **excess cost `L=C-24`**, never average individual payoff. The following *decision rule* is entirely hypothetical; it is NOT the author's SSM5/SSMq, not calibrated to participants, and does not identify experimental human cognition.

## 2. Legal information and finite-state behavioral assumptions

All six synthetic actors see only **previous period's publicly disclosed n_A** plus their own previous route before choosing a simultaneous next action: this is a stylized FULL-information feedback regime; it is NOT legal as a cognitive input for original uninformed actors. Each actor evaluates a unilateral move against that old state, not the realized next state:

`g_A(n)=c_A(n)-c_B(7-n)=3n-14`; `g_B(n)=c_B(6-n)-c_A(n+1)=11-3n`.

With subjective threshold kappa=0, the per-actor *conditional-on-revision-clock* switching probabilities are `a(n)=2/5` if `g_A>0` else `1/50`, `b(n)=2/5` if `g_B>0` else `1/50`. `1/50` is an artificial exploration/error floor, required for escape and not fitted to human noise. Let revision speed `eta∈[0,1]`, shared-clock probability `theta=1/2`.

- Independent revisions: each A actor flips with `theta*eta*a(n)`, each B actor with `theta*eta*b(n)`, independently.
- Common revision clock: `R_t~Bernoulli(theta)`, shared among actors. Conditional on R_t, each A flips with `R_t*eta*a(n)`, each B with `R_t*eta*b(n)`, independently.
- **Every individual has EXACTLY the same marginal probability conditional on the previous n** under both clocks. Only joint correlation is changed. Neither is a policy assigned in the actual 24 sessions.

The occupancy `n'=n-D+U` has a seven-state transition kernel: `D~Binom(n,p_A)`, `U~Binom(6-n,p_B)`, conditional independent as specified. Original six-person individual labels were separately enumerated over all 64 route masks as an independent implementation check; **lumpability** follows from route-type symmetry. All 7x7 Markov calculations use Python `Fraction`, with no Monte Carlo sampling.

## 3. Exact results: local sign reverses after only 2 transitions

The one-step group covariance identity for original social excess `L=3(n-4)^2` is

`D_1(n) = 3*theta*(1-theta)*eta² * [((6-n)*b(n)-n*a(n))² - (n*a(n)²+(6-n)*b(n)²)]`

where D1 denotes **common minus independent** conditional expected next-step L. This is a direct application of a standard quadratic variance identity (NOT claimed as new). At old n=4, `a=b=1/50`, so exactly

`D1(4)=-6*theta*(1-theta)*eta²*(1/50)²=-3/5000=-0.000600`

for eta=1,theta=1/2. **Public common-clock updating has slightly lower conditional social congestion at this starting state**, even though individual revision marginals and expected immediate gross revisions are identical.

However with feedback and self-generated occupancy over time, `D_H(n_0) = sum_{t=0}^{H-1} e_n0*(P_common^t-P_independent^t)*L`:

- `D_H(4)`, H=2 periods: **-0.000600** exactly.
- H=3 periods: **+0.063864312104652** exactly by Fraction (`15966078026163/250000000000000`); sign already reversed.
- H=40 periods: **+24.935384812017** (common worse). Independent cumulative excess 18.257639; common 43.193024. At t=39, P(n=4) independent 0.865252 vs common 0.760558.
- Expected number of real route changes over 39 transitions: independent 5.759229, common 8.474830; their first-state gross-change expectations are equal. Subsequent n distributions differ.

At old n=5, common has even one-step worse excess **+2.34**; H40 common-minus-independent **+44.424358**. Therefore the local sign is **state dependent** and does not imply the cumulative sign.

This is a **constructive counterexample to lifting a first-step welfare covariance sign into an unrestricted long-horizon sign**. Not a claim that all common clocks worsen congestion or Full-information feedback generally increases social loss.

## 4. Robustness within an openly delimited behavioral family

The initial efficient state n0=4 was compared over an independent 27-cell grid: favorable switching rate `{1/5,2/5,3/5}` × exploration error rate `{1/100,1/50,1/25}` × public clock probability `{1/4,1/2,3/4}`, retaining the same threshold structure. All **27/27** cases had lower H=2 common-clock excess; all **27/27** had higher H=40 common-clock excess. Across these 27 cases, H40 gap ranged from **+2.787906 to +104.849980**. This is robustness across a chosen grid, **not a global sign theorem**, not statistically sampled and not externally validated on people.

## 5. Revision inertia and genuine social-resource switching fees

Within independent revisions and n0=5, lowering revision speed eta has a **nonmonotonic** 40-period effect:

| eta | H40 excess congestion | Expected gross revisions |
|---|---:|---:|
| 0.05 | 57.209005 | 0.983721 |
| 0.10 | 38.041131 | 1.337332 |
| 0.20 | 26.917184 | 1.896929 |
| 0.30 | 23.726898 | 2.475100 |
| 0.40 | **22.620498** | 3.086125 |
| 0.60 | 22.728775 | 4.423094 |
| 0.80 | 24.370918 | 5.943222 |
| 1.00 | 27.093566 | 7.687804 |

These results are not a model of psychological disutility measured in money. Subjective threshold kappa and reduced revision propensity eta change *decision rules*. The **actual resource switching fee f** is an external real social loss per completed switch, so exact horizon-H resource-inclusive excess is

`J_H(n0; f)= E[sum_{t=0}^{H-1} L(n_t)] + f E[sum_{t=0}^{H-2} (D_t+U_t)]`.

The payoff-policy choice `eta=0.4` beats `eta=0.3` for n0=5 when f=0; their exact crossover occurs at **f≈1.810727747** units per actual route switch. At f=2, `eta=0.3` beats `eta=0.4`; on the tested eight-point grid the optimal eta shifts from 0.4 (f=0) to 0.3 (f=2), and 0.2 at f=10. No global continuous optimum or human fee estimate is claimed. At efficient n0=4, slower revision is better across the same grid; reducing a low but positive error hazard reduces departures, whereas n0=5 initially needs adjustment.

This is the RITHM **micro -> macro sign problem**: the welfare impact of individual effort suppression depends jointly on initial congestion, correlated clock structure, feedback horizon, and physical switching resources.

## 6. Caveats and rival mechanisms

1. Fixed rate `2/5`, error `1/50`, theta=1/2 and discrete eta grid are synthetic scenario assumptions; no fit to any original human transitions, EWA, or original SSM5.
2. Knowledge of previous n is available only to designated informed actors. No use of observed future n or next-period payoffs. No transfer to No/Partial/Endogenous treatment as participant cognition without valid information masks.
3. Seven-state occupancy projection discards actor identity, payoff-memory, voluntary information selection and exact-configuration age. The T4 individual/selection rivalry remains OPEN.
4. Finite H=40, particular original six-player cost function. The first-step covariance identity is classical; state-dependent inertia and simultaneous overshooting are established prior-art themes, including [Jiang et al. 2026](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ins.2026.123817), [Sandholm (2010)](https://mitpress.ublish.com/book/population-games-and-evolutionary-dynamics), and [Li, Wang & Nie (2024)](https://doi.org/10.1287/trsc.2023.0132). No novelty priority claimed.
5. T5 original dataset **not re-fitted or reanalyzed**. Source-free synthetic results do not establish a common revision clock among experiment participants or any intervention's causal value.
