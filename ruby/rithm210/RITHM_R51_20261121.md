# P3-R51 — Incomplete rehearsal logs and first-revision bounds

2026-11-21 · Operational rehearsal with synthetic groups

## Observation rule

R50 compared first post-settlement focal revisions by delay and recorded when a
completed group had no focal opportunity. R51 takes one read-only snapshot of
the R49 SQLite event ledger, including every allocation and every valid prefix.
The export uses pseudonymous group references and omits live receipt tokens.
The original completed export and participant interface use the same protocol as
before. The unrestricted schedule makes the roster suitable for operator access
after a freeze, since it includes each group's future revision clock.

The endpoint is a switch at the focal participant's first scheduled revision
after round two and within the assigned horizon, under full continuation. A
committed first action determines that outcome even if the group stops later.
A stored clock with no such round determines a zero for this endpoint and is
counted on its own row. A scheduled first action without a committed route
remains unresolved. Groups stopping before the atomic second choice and
settlement assignment enter the allocation tally with their last committed round.
The signed treatment denominator starts once the frame and sign are assigned,
including when settlement has yet to be issued or acknowledged.

## Exact example

All groups use the 5:1 initial split, 5 rounds and the route frame. The frozen
snapshot has 14 allocations. Two stop before signed assignment, one at round
zero and one after round one.

| Signed amount | Assigned | Complete | First switch | First stay | No scheduled focal revision | Unresolved |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| −4 | 6 | 2 | 2 | 1 | 1 | 2 |
| +4 | 6 | 2 | 2 | 2 | 0 | 2 |

The two completed −4 groups both switch and the two completed +4 groups both
stay. The complete-case contrast is +100 percentage points. The two +4
switches and the −4 group with no scheduled opportunity are all incomplete.

For N signed assignments, s known switches and u unresolved actions, the
fully observed rate lies in [s/N, (s+u)/N]. For the −4 minus +4 contrast,
use the lower endpoint from −4 minus the upper from +4 and the upper from
−4 minus the lower from +4. Here both rate intervals are [1/3, 2/3] and the
contrast is [−1/3, +1/3]. The executable witness continues two copies of the
same frozen SQLite ledger through the original state machine, retains every
committed event and assigned clock, and attains both endpoints by assigning
opposite first actions to the unresolved groups. These are finite-set
missing-outcome bounds for the synthetic fixture. Human treatment and welfare
effects would need their own study evidence and sampling uncertainty.

## Reproduce

Run from repository root against an operator-controlled database. The demo
creates and discards its own synthetic database.

```sh
python ruby/rithm210/p3/r49_rehearsal/r51_roster.py path/to/r49_rehearsal.sqlite3 > roster.json
python ruby/rithm210/p3/r49_rehearsal/r51_audit_roster.py roster.json
python ruby/rithm210/p3/r49_rehearsal/r51_demo.py
python -m unittest discover -s ruby/rithm210/p3/r49_rehearsal
```

Carry the same allocation and completeness accounting into cumulative resource
cost. A first revision can be known while most of its downstream occupancy
path is missing, so a sharp result for first revision can coexist with wide
bounds on repeated congestion cost.
