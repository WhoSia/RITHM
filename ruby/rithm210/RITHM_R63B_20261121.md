# RITHM-2.10 P3-R63B — Legal Information-Bridge Transport and 40-Round Closed-Loop Rank Reversal

**2026-11-21 · RITHM 28 · P3-R63B-P1/P2 completed original-data retrospective analysis; NO external validation, causal policy effect or novel-general-theorem promotion.**

## 1. Why this study follows R63A

R63A showed that lower pooled 40-period congestion MSE does not establish fidelity to a No-vs-Full treatment contrast. R63B now asks a narrower predictive transport question: Can individual decision probabilities trained under No and Full be applied to Partial, whose six-person groups contain EXACTLY three uninformed and three informed actors, without using analyst-only occupancy? Is any one-step advantage preserved when the forecast itself generates all 40-period routes and payoffs?

Research is based on the owner-authorized original [Noussair–Qiao, Congestion Information and Efficiency: An Experiment](https://doi.org/10.1287/mnsc.2023.00056) ZIP, pinned SHA256 `e6adce2da4d1c01455e912aa15144f192108394cbec0bd7f767e2732dcec9953`. ZIP remains in its original restricted Drive Data Room; no person/ID/session trajectory/Value tape is published. All original data analysis was executed LOCALLY; GitHub CI is source-free.

## 2. Cohort and information-lawful method

Prior R60/R61/R62 population maintained exactly: 24 original sessions, sections 3 and 4, periods 2–40, 96 six-actor group-sections, 3,744 group transitions and 22,464 actor-change decisions; sessions 2 and 101 excluded. Of the 24 sessions, 17 contain Partial-target group-sections, totaling 32 Partial groups, 1,248 target group transitions and 7,488 actors' decisions.

Across ALL 1,248 Partial-target transitions, three participants have info=0 and three have info=1. Original No has info=0, Full info=1. Thus direct No→Full transport is not supported nonparametrically after conditioning on the original information mask; Partial enables matched-mask source comparisons but does not make treatment and information policy causally exchangeable.

Two predictive competitors:

- **No+Full BRIDGE:** for a held-out Partial group, fit uninformed individual predictions exclusively on No actors in the other 23 original sessions; fit informed predictions exclusively on Full actors in those 23 sessions. ZERO Partial outcomes are used to fit either source probability.
- **Partial INVARIANT comparator:** fit both information-mask-specific predictions exclusively on Partial actors in the other 23 sessions. A held-out source session is excluded entirely from fitting, not merely its target group.

Both models use only a personally possible predecision history: own previous route, prior-section own switch count, current-section own earlier switches, own received previous payoff and current own route-streak length. No previous whole-group occupancy is supplied as a personal cognitive predictor, and no contemporaneous or future Value(t) is used in a choice. Base by (own info mask, own previous route) is smoothed Beta(.5,.5); previous memory uses inherited fixed tau=8 and shrink=20; 3 prior-own-payoff bins × 3 route-tenure bins give source-only logit calibration. All 9 bins under both information masks have at least 30 SOURCE training actors in every evaluated fold. This is coarse-stratum support only, not continuous covariate overlap.

## 4. P2: Matched 40-round endogenous choice/payoff rollouts

For each of 32 Partial group-sections, 64 coupled simulations are generated with both competitors using exactly the same six-person uniform random draws. Every period all six actors decide simultaneously from the previous own state; occupancy is recomputed AFTER all decisions and realized own payoff is calculated from the newly chosen route and contemporaneous exogenous Value(t), never supplied to the actor before that choice. Original actual t1 routes are fixed in the primary run; fair 50:50 t1 and independent seed 20261123 are sensitivity runs. Group total excess congestion is Σ_(t=1)^40 3(nA_t−4)^2; MSE is mean squared forecast error against the historical total of each original Partial group (NOT an estimated causal effect).

| Partial-target 40-round group-total MSE ↓ | No+Full bridge | Partial-only fitted |
|---|---:|---:|
| Observed t1, seed 20261121 | **4648.135** | 5546.812 |
| Observed t1, seed 20261123 | **4739.098** | 5558.273 |
| Fair t1, seed 20261121 | **5857.989** | 6771.318 |

The pooled-group improvement in the observed-start primary run is −898.677 MSE (about 16.2% of comparator). The paired **17-session equal-weight** MSE difference is −794.508, cluster descriptive 95% [−1590.245,−152.905]. Second seed: equal-session delta −1013.847, [−1641.254,−490.552]. This does NOT prove stable information-policy mechanisms; all compared models retain severe collective-stability and mean-congestion errors, with no direct external sample.

## 5. Source and methodological boundaries

- Read-only source is the **author-approved** original ZIP; its SHA is checked before parsing and participant rows never appear in public artifacts.
- No/Full differ in information availability by design. The bridge maps ONLY like-information masks into Partial. This prevents an illegal actor-oracle feature; it does not identify the treatment effect or separate all group contextual differences.
- Participants' previous own payoff and their previous own route history are allowed in the model. The original recorded future routes are used only to compute historical aggregate reference totals, not generated choices.
- Every one-step fold excludes the entire held-out original session, and training of the bridge uses NO Partial records at all. Nevertheless the overall research design has substantial post hoc model exposure to the same cohort.
- The 40-period predicted group MSE is distinct from one-period joint U,D logloss, gross switching, no-switch persistence and group efficiency; report all independently.
- Any relation to strategic cooperation, latent shared state, real psychological habit, actual resource cost kappa or policy social welfare remains unverified. No new participant recruitment, no private source re-publication.
