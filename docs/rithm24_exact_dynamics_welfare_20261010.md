## 1. Complete phase diagram under restrictive synchronous mean-field assumptions

At b=0: (i) |theta|<=1 globally attracts the sole zero equilibrium; (ii) theta>1 admits two attracting equilibria ±r and repelling zero; (iii) theta<−1 attracts a sign-alternating 2-cycle ±r for every nonzero initial state, with r=tanh(|theta|r). Classical results from monotonicity and concavity of tanh. Do not transfer to asynchronous/discrete human networks without proof.
For theta>1 the fold thresholds for quasistatic guidance are ±bc, with s=sqrt(1−1/theta) and bc=theta*s−atanh(s); theta=1.5 yields r=0.8585596366401104 and bc=0.2075464553220303.

## 2. Exact cost-observability counterexample
Two symmetric roads with per-driver cost equal to current road load, total mass N=12, have total cost C(z)=72(1+z²). With θ=+1.5 and z0=r, the occupancy imbalance remains +r forever; with θ=−1.5 and the SAME z0, imbalance alternates ±r forever. **Total traffic cost is identical each and every period**, 125.072974776. Lag-one signed flow products are +r² versus −r². Even an arbitrarily long cost-only sequence cannot identify these two mechanisms under the stated cost symmetry; temporally ordered route counts can. A population mixture of ±r stationary basins and random-phase 2-cycle has identical one-period route-share distributions but different path laws.

## 4. Delay and structural welfare lemmas
Delayed logit z(t+1)=tanh(a z(t)+d z(t−1)+b(t)) has local linear characteristic polynomial λ²−aλ−d. By the classical Schur/Jury criterion zero is strictly locally stable iff all three inequalities hold: 1−a−d>0, 1+a−d>0, 1+d>0. For the SAME static gain a+d=.6: (.6,0) is stable (spectral radius .6); (1.7,−1.1) is unstable (spectral radius sqrt(1.1)). This says memory placement affects local stability; NOT a global nonlinear stability theorem.
For |theta|<1 and small constant guidance b, steady state z*(b)=tanh(theta z*(b)+b) has z*'(0)=1/(1−theta); symmetric cost C=72(1+z*²) has second derivative 144/(1−theta)²>0 at b=0. A fixed one-way biased guidance moves choices but locally worsens total congestion cost near the balanced optimum. State-dependent corrective guidance may differ.
