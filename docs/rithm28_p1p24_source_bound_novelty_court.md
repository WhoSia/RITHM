# RITHM P1-P24 — Four Primary Papers, Novelty Collision and Exact Price-Grid Court

Date 2026-10-19. Original project name unchanged. **Four source PDFs newly received in Drive 00_INTAKE, text checked, renamed/moved preserving file ID to 10_PAPERS** (https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1D2M4LHcejREhlyp6vTd71xxLLx-6ldUP):

1. [Castiglioni, Celli, Marchesi & Gatti 2021, AAAI paper](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1Q_gnVE4965ItxDlxJbA0X32oaaT-AK5f/view): *Signaling in Bayesian Network Congestion Games: The Subtle Power of Symmetry*. Theorem 1: for symmetric atomic Bayesian network congestion games, optimize ex-ante persuasive signaling in polytime; Theorem 2: asymmetric case NP-hard even affine costs. **Not** privately purchased signal model. Novelty barrier for generic 'computing signaling optimum under congestion is hard'.
2. [Griesbach, Hoefer, Klimm & Koglin 2024, arXiv v1](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1uv8yORm31d3GfSv7-_ubf102GQv6M-tG/view): *Information Design for Congestion Games with Unknown Demand*. Public principal signal; nonatomic Wardrop equilibrium, affine networks. FPTAS for two demand states and full revelation optimal in series-parallel class; not the RITHM three atomic agents / individually purchased costly observation game. State 'non-atomic' correctly.
3. [Das, Kamenica & Mirka author manuscript](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1p7qEO5kAq7HyZg933vQ00Lz34MR6dNvR/view): *Reducing Congestion Through Information Design*. Bayesian persuasion with partial signal design for congestion reduction; example continuum travelers and state-varying uncongested option. Merely 'partial information beats full information' is old.
4. [Zeng, Huang, He & Cai, SSRN 4789552 preprint](https://drive.google.com/file/d/12-Q6ZXxU3JeUQfeX4fCAj2flQCTPU1lX/view): *Joint Information Design and Pricing for Transportation Information Service*. **Crucial novelty conflict:** joint information pricing, endogenous subscribers, information value depending on subscriber mass; Proposition 1 gives a threshold g(pi) after which subscribers fail to exploit signal and act like uninformed travellers. This paper explicitly handles costly private information/subscription and congestion. Do NOT claim 'costly observation plus congestion' novel. Preprint itself identifies as *not peer reviewed*; publisher status must be audited separately.

## New model check
[Exact rational q-grid pure Nash court](../ruby/rithm28/p1p24/price_frontier.rb) + [test](../ruby/rithm28/p1p24/test_price_frontier.rb) scan q=j/16, 0<=j<=16, holding k=3/4,p=1/2, three agents with six contingent plans each, 216 pure profiles each q. 17*216=3672 full pure profile evaluations (and six unilateral deviations each). Records pure equilibrium count, best/worst all-in expected social cost and if any pure equilibrium exceeds uninspected baseline 6. This does NOT certify intervals between grid points and does NOT compute mixed equilibria. It is a finite falsifier/checker, not a novelty result.

## Direction change after primary reading
P1-P23 three pure equilibria and welfare heterogeneity remain useful examples but not a prima facie publication-level novelty: existing papers, especially Zeng et al., directly investigate subscribers' value erosion under congestion. Proposed sharp gap to investigate is *algorithm-aware endogenous acquisition* under observation/legal clocks with finite bounded computation and policy friction, avoiding the regression 'who decides which computations to execute'. Formal non-transport applications require actual homomorphism and invariant transfer proof, not mere analogies. Build lower-bound / algorithmic limits and rival tests before claiming theorem.
